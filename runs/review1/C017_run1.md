## Issues for the controller

1. **Uninstalled materials inflate revenue.** The cost to date includes £5,716,000 of bought-in steel, spools and valves that are on site or in the bonded store and not yet installed (2026-12-17_memo_procurement-status.md; 2027-01-21_minutes_contract-review.md). Under IFRS 15.B19 these should be excluded from the progress measure. Effect: revenue is overstated by about £6.58m (team basis on my TP). If the controller concludes that control of the materials has passed to the customer and the materials are distinct, IFRS 15.B20 could support recognising them at cost with no margin.
