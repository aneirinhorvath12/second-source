## 2. Health, safety and environment

55,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Marlow Insulation will provide feedback at the next meeting.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 300 Utilities. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Ashlar Scaffolding has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Gareth Moffat will sample the lifting records next week.

## 7. Quality

Coating dry film thickness readings were taken on the external surfaces of the 900 mm diameter pipeline sections at the stockyard. Readings were logged at every metre along each section in accordance with the specification. Two sections showed thin areas near the girth weld ends and will be overcoated after the surface is lightly abraded.

Please keep the route to the hydrant open so that a fire tender can reach it within two minutes of an alarm.
