## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

The LOLER thorough examination of the 25 tonne mobile crane is due next Thursday, and the current certificate was checked at the meeting. The hook block and wire rope inspection records are complete, but the outrigger pad condition report is still outstanding from the hire company. Until it is received, the crane will only be used within the limits of the existing load chart, and lifts will be planned accordingly.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Kieran Gallagher will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 5. Design and technical

9 RFIs open, 6 closed in the period.

A design check of the sump pump sizing for the process area showed that the calculated flow rate is slightly higher than the figure on the datasheet. The mechanical engineer has asked the vendor to confirm whether the selected pump still meets the head requirement at the higher flow. A response is expected before the datasheet is updated.

## 7. Quality

Non-destructive testing reports for the pressure vessel nozzles were issued by the independent inspection body. Magnetic particle testing found no relevant indications on the attachment welds. The reports have been checked against the weld map and signed off by Kieran Gallagher, so the vessel can move to the next stage.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Batching plant drivers must use the designated wash-out area and must not discharge into the surface water drains. The banksman will guide each mixer into position, and the site engineer will sign the delivery tickets once the slump has been checked.
