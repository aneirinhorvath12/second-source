## 2. Health, safety and environment

33,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Segregated walkways and vehicle routes have been reviewed around the new tanker bay. Delivery drivers were seen reversing without a banksman on two occasions last week. A second banksman will be rostered during peak delivery hours, and yellow lines have been repainted at the turning circle. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Claire Quigley has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.
