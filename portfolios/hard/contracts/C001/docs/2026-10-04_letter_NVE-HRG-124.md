I write to issue Employer's Representative's Instruction ERI-032 under clause 14 of the Contract.

**Instruction**

You are instructed to provide an additional access road and turning head for the Employer's operations team, as described below.

**Description of the change**

- The road is to leave the existing site perimeter road at the north-east corner of the compressor compound and run approximately 180 m along the northern boundary to a turning head adjacent to the future operations building.
- Carriageway width is to be 4.5 m with a sealed surface suitable for 26-tonne rigid vehicles. The turning head is to accommodate a 12 m rigid vehicle without reversing more than once.
- Surface water drainage is to be connected to the existing attenuation system. Any necessary adjustments to the existing drainage layout are to be included in your proposals.

The Employer's operations team has asked that the road is usable before the first commissioning walkdowns. Please take this into account when you consider sequencing.
