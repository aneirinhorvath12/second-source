This letter is the Employer's Representative's Instruction **ERI-029**, issued under clause 14 of the Contract.

**Instruction**

You are instructed to provide extra noise attenuation enclosures for the compressors at Halden Ridge, in addition to the enclosures currently shown in the design.

**Technical detail**

- The enclosures are to be provided for each of the compressor trains, and are to be designed to achieve a further reduction in the sound pressure level measured at the site boundary.
- The enclosures must allow maintenance access to the compressor casings and drivers without full removal of panels, and must not obstruct the existing lifting arrangements.
- Ventilation and gas detection provisions within each enclosure are to be reviewed and, where necessary, modified to suit the revised arrangement. The interface with the fire and gas system will need to be confirmed.

**Quotation**

Please submit your quotation under clause 14.2 within 14 days of the date of this letter. The quotation should set out the proposed change to the programme, if any, together with the supporting build-up of the price. Please quote the instruction reference ERI-029 on your submission.
