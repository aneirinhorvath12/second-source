**Subject:** Halden Ridge Gas Compression O&M manuals: agreed format and submission schedule

Each manual will be issued as a single PDF per system, with a master index in the front section. The structure will be:

- Section 1: system description, process flow diagram and key operating parameters
- Section 2: start-up, normal operation and shutdown procedures
- Section 3: alarm and trip schedules, with setpoints taken from the approved cause and effect matrices
- Section 4: preventive maintenance routines, lubrication schedules and spares lists
- Section 5: vendor documentation, bound in as appendices in their original form
- Section 6: certificates, test records and inspection reports

Drawing references will follow the document numbering already agreed for the project, and every page will carry the revision number and issue date. Vendor data sheets will be cross-referenced rather than reproduced in full, except where the customer's maintenance team has asked for a complete copy.

The submission schedule is as follows. Draft manuals for the gas compression package will be issued for comment on the first working day of next month, with the water treatment and substation manuals to follow two weeks later. Comments should be returned within ten working days so that final issue can be made before commissioning.

If your team has a preferred layout for the alarm schedules, please send an example and we will align the template. Regards to all.
