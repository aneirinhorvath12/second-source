## 2. Health, safety and environment

23,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

## 5. Design and technical

11 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

Model review of the compressor building was carried out with Neil Kerr and Kieran Gallagher present. Walkways and access routes were tested against the maintenance envelope requirements for the reciprocating machine. Two platforms were found to restrict lifting access, and the design team will propose revised steelwork to restore clear routes.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 400 Storage identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Ian Fraser so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.
