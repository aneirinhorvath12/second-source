## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

41,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

A technical query from Marlow Insulation concerns the material specification for the gaskets on the sulphuric acid line. The drawing calls for a PTFE-based gasket, but the purchase specification lists a graphite option. Engineering has been asked to confirm the chemical compatibility and to issue a single approved specification to both teams.

## 7. Quality

A welder qualification record for one operative on Corvan Piping Services was found to have expired the previous Friday. The welder was removed from production welding immediately and the weld log was checked back against the record. Two root runs completed during the lapse have been flagged for additional ultrasonic testing.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The insulated pipe spools for Area 400 Storage are arriving in six separate loads over the next three days. Each load needs a pre-booked slot at the goods reception cabin. Drivers must bring the delivery note, the material certificate copy and the photo ID that matches the booking. Late arrivals will be held at the weighbridge until a slot is free.
