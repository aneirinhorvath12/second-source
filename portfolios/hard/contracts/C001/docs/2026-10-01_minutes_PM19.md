## 2. Health, safety and environment

43,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Ian Fraser will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.
