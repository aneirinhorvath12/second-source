Further to our site walk on Tuesday, I am writing to confirm the customer's requirements for the perimeter hoarding along the Area 400 Storage frontage and the east access road.

The customer's branding panels are to be fixed at 2.4 metre centres on the outer face only, with the approved logo panels set at eye level. Please note the following:

- Panels are to be supplied in the approved brand palette and carry the document reference from the customer's identity manual.
- Hoarding sheets that have been scuffed or damaged by wind are to be replaced, not overpainted.
- Gate panels at the main access and the pedestrian gate are to show the project name and the emergency contact number, not the general enquiry line.
- Panels must be kept clear of vehicle sightlines at the junction with the public highway, as agreed with the local authority.

Brackmoor's team should provide a fixing schedule and a photograph of each elevation once the panels are up. I would be grateful if you could confirm the installation programme by the end of next week so that Ruth Strachan can arrange sign-off on the artwork.
