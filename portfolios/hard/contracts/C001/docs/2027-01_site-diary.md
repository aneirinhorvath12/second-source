### Monday 04 January 2027
**Weather:** Dry and bright. 9°C.
**Labour:** Brackmoor 20; Corvan Piping Services 16; Ashlar Scaffolding 6; Marlow Insulation 19; total 61.
**Plant:** 2 x concrete pump, 3 x 25t excavator, 2 x 100t crawler crane.
**Work progressed:**

**Deliveries:** 2 x ready-mix concrete, 3 x grating panels.
**Notes:** Lockout tagout audit on the compressor house. Three isolation points were found without the correct padlock colour coding, and the isolation certificate for the gas line was missing a signature. Supervisor corrected both during the walk. Refresher reminder sent to all authorised persons in the area.
**HSE:** Toolbox talk: slips and trips. No incidents.

### Tuesday 05 January 2027
**Weather:** Light rain am, dry pm. 12°C.

- Area 200 Process - Piping - Hydrotest (part 3 of 3)
- Area 500 Export - Piping - Install spools (part 1 of 3)
**Visitors:** Ruth Strachan.
**Notes:** Hydrotest of the 6-inch firewater header completed on the north run. Pressure held at 12 barg for the full ten-minute hold with no visible weeping at the flanges. Test pump was isolated and drained, and the gauge calibration sticker was checked against the test pack. Results sent to Gareth Moffat for sign-off this afternoon.
