**Subject:** Attempted theft of copper earth cable, cable drum store, 9 May

At 02:40 on Friday 9 May, the night security patrol found a section of the south perimeter fence cut open beside the cable drum store. A length of 70mm2 copper earth cable had been partly cut from a drum and pulled towards the fence. The patrol saw two individuals leaving along the access road with their vehicle lights off, and they did not stop when challenged.

The cable was not removed from site. The cut section is approximately three metres long and has been retained for inspection. The police attended at 03:15 and have issued crime reference 4417/0509. A copy of the reference is attached.

Actions taken so far:

- Fence breach made secure with temporary Heras panels and a fence inspection logged
- Temporary lighting installed along the south fence and at the cable store
- Patrol frequency increased to every 90 minutes overnight
- CCTV camera at gate 2 repositioned to cover the drum store approach
- Full inventory check of cable drums completed on Friday morning, with no other losses found

I will arrange a permanent fence repair with Marlow Insulation for next week and will send the completed inspection report on Monday. Could you confirm whether the customer's security team wishes to review the incident log before we close it out?
