**Subject:** Fennick Hydrogen Pilot: your variation VO-002

Dear Rhys,

I write as Employer's Representative for the Fennick Hydrogen Pilot contract, following receipt of your submission for variation VO-002, which proposes a valuation of £1,212,726 for the replacement of the security fencing around the pilot compound and the associated access control points.

I have reviewed your submission against the Employer's Requirements and the drawings and specification issued at tender, together with the clarifications agreed before contract signature. The perimeter security provisions, including the removal and replacement of the existing fencing, are described in the Employer's Requirements under the site security section and are shown on the tender drawings issued with the contract documents. The works you describe are within the original scope of the contract and do not constitute a change to it.

Your submission refers to the condition of the existing fence line as found on mobilisation. The condition survey that your team carried out during the tender period was available to you before the contract was signed. Your submission does not identify any instruction from me, or any change to the Employer's Requirements, that altered the fencing scope after that date.

VO-002 is therefore **not accepted**. I will not be issuing an instruction in respect of it, and no adjustment to the contract sum or the programme is agreed on the basis of your submission. Please proceed with the fencing works as part of the contracted scope, in line with the current programme and the agreed sequence for the compound handover.

If you believe there is a specific element of the fencing works that falls outside the Employer's Requirements, please identify it by reference to the relevant requirement and drawing, and explain how it differs from what was described at tender. I will consider any such submission on its merits, but it should be made as a separate item and not folded into a general resubmission of VO-002.

I would be happy to meet on site to walk the fence line with your site team and with our security consultant, should that help to establish a shared understanding of what is covered. Please let me know a convenient date in the next fortnight and I will arrange attendance.

Yours sincerely,
