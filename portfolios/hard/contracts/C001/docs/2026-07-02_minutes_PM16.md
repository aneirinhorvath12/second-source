## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

42,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Gareth Moffat has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 7. Quality

Three gauges drifted outside tolerance and have been withdrawn, with replacements fitted and recorded on the calibration log held by Gareth Moffat.
