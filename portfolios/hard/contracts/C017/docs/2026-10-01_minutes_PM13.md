## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Stuart Dunmore has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.

## 5. Design and technical

14 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

A model clash review covering the chemical dosing area highlighted interference between a cable tray and a new sample line. The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Coating dry film thickness readings were taken on the external surfaces of the 900 mm diameter pipeline sections at the stockyard. Readings were logged at every metre along each section in accordance with the specification. Two sections showed thin areas near the girth weld ends and will be overcoated after the surface is lightly abraded.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

A water main shutdown has been agreed with the local supplier for Tuesday between ten and two. Hydrants on the north side will be unavailable during this window, so the fire marshals need to be told. Please fill any hand-wash stations and check the site toilet cisterns before the shutdown begins, as the supply will be off.

## 9. Any other business

Nitrogen purge bottles used for the pipework closures have been left uncapped in several places. The cylinder store team has been asked to chain all bottles to the rack at the end of each shift. Any cylinder found standing unsecured will be returned to the supplier and replaced on the next delivery.
