## 2. Health, safety and environment

57,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Harness inspection records for Orrin Commissioning show that eleven fall arrest harnesses were removed from service during the month because of frayed stitching or damaged buckles. Replacements have been issued and the old harnesses have been cut up and disposed of. Each worker must present their harness for a pre-use check before going onto the pipe rack, and supervisors will log the checks on the daily permit.

The scaffold on the south side of the separator vessel was inspected and tagged green following the recent modifications. The inspector noted that a toe board was missing on one lift and a ladder access was positioned incorrectly. Both points were corrected before the tag was signed. Users should check the tag before access and report any damage or loose fittings immediately to the scaffold foreman.
