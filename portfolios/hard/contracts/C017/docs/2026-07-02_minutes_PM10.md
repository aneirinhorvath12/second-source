## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

53,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

Harness inspection records for Marlow Insulation show that eleven fall arrest harnesses were removed from service during the month because of frayed stitching or damaged buckles. Replacements have been issued and the old harnesses have been cut up and disposed of. Each worker must present their harness for a pre-use check before going onto the pipe rack, and supervisors will log the checks on the daily permit.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Stuart Dunmore will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 5. Design and technical

5 RFIs open, 4 closed in the period.

A model clash review covering the chemical dosing area highlighted interference between a cable tray and a new sample line. The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 600 Control Building identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

Please keep the lifting zone clear of all personnel and vehicles from six in the morning until the bundle is landed and secured.
