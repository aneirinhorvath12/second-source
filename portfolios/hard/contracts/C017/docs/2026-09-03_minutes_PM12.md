## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

47,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Portable appliance testing on the temporary power distribution boards is now 90 per cent complete. Three extension leads failed their insulation resistance test and were removed from site. Any lead that is damaged or has missing earthing must be taken out of use immediately and reported to the electrical supervisor. Test labels must be checked before tools are issued.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Stuart Dunmore has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.
