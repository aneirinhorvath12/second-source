# Internal memo

**To:** Marcus Yates, Commercial Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd; Stuart Dunmore, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Callum Hartley, Senior Quantity Surveyor
**Date:** 17 December 2026
**Subject:** Procurement status: materials on site

Marcus, Stuart,

This note sets out where we stand on the early-purchased materials, so that the cost report for the period and the progress figures are read together.

**What has been bought**

Early in the job we bought structural steel, pipe spools and valves ahead of the programme in order to secure prices. Deliveries are now in. A total of £5,716,000 of materials is on site or in the bonded store and has not yet been installed. Neil Morgan's team did a walk-down of the laydown area and the bonded store last week, and the stock lists agree with the goods-received records. Some of the steel is still on the original delivery bearers, and I understand Neil is looking at re-stacking it before the January weather.

**How it shows in the cost report**

The materials are costed to the job on receipt, so the cost to date includes the full £5,716,000. This is simply our normal practice for bought-in materials, and no adjustment has been made to it.

**How it shows in progress**

Physical progress, and therefore earned value, only counts the materials once they are installed. Until then they add to cost but not to progress. Anyone comparing cost to date against earned value will therefore see cost running ahead of progress by a margin that reflects this stock. That is a timing matter and will reduce as the steel is erected and the spools and valves are fitted.

**Effect on the estimate to complete**

The remaining budget in the ETC has been reduced by the same amount, £5,716,000, because the materials are already paid for and we will not be buying them again. The ETC therefore only carries the cost of installing them, not of purchasing them.

**For your attention**

- When the monthly figures are discussed, please keep the three points above together, as the cost and progress lines will otherwise look out of step.
- I will keep a running schedule of materials received against materials installed, so the balance can be tracked each month. I will issue the first version with the January cost report.

Let me know if you would like the schedule broken down by package before then.
