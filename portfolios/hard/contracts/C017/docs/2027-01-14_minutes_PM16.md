# Ovington LNG Loading Arm: progress meeting no. 16

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Gareth Thornton (ER), Bethan Ellison, Rhys Talbot, Aisha Fraser (Sorrel Renewables); Stuart Dunmore, Marcus Yates, Neil Morgan, Euan Bryant (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** Callum Hartley

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

54,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Sorrel Renewables representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 48.3%. Activities progressed this period: Area 200 Process - Electrical - Install cable tray (part 2 of 3); Area 200 Process - Piping - Install spools (part 2 of 2); Area 100 Inlet - Electrical - Install cable tray (part 1 of 3); Area 300 Utilities - Piping - Fabricate spools (part 2 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 05 July 2028 against contract completion 14 October 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 4 closed in the period.

Structural steel connection details for the pipe rack in Area 300 Utilities were queried by the erection team. Several gusset plates appear to conflict with the revised beam sizes shown in the latest general arrangement. The structural engineer has agreed to reissue the connection schedule, and erectors will hold work on those bays until the new details are confirmed.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 16 submitted on 06 January 2027.

## 7. Quality

A toolbox talk was held on the importance of hold point signatures and the consequences of closing work before inspection. Foremen were reminded that a hold point is a stop, not a formality. Attendance was recorded and the sheet was filed with the weekly safety and quality pack.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.

The fire marshals need to be told in advance so that the sprinkler cover can be arranged for the temporary office block. Neil Morgan to confirm with the fire service that the hydrant will be available and that the test will be witnessed.
