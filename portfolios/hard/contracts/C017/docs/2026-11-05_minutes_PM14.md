## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

56,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 7. Quality

Dimensional checks on the skid frame for the chemical dosing package were recorded against the approved fabrication drawings. Base plate levels were within tolerance, but the nozzle orientation on one vessel was rotated by about four degrees. The fabricator has been asked to submit a proposed correction for review by the design team.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Traffic management for the Ashby Road closure needs confirmation before the pipe trench is opened. The temporary traffic lights must be set to run on the approved sequence, and the signage must be checked each morning. Stuart Dunmore has asked the banksman team to log any near misses and report back before the end of each shift.

## 9. Any other business

Crane mats for the lift on the compressor skid were found cracked at the edges during Monday's inspection. The damaged mats have been removed from the laydown area and tagged. Replacement mats are due on site next Tuesday. Stuart Dunmore asked that the lift plan be reissued once the new mats are in place, with the ground bearing calculation attached for the lifting supervisor's sign-off.
