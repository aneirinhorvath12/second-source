## 2. Health, safety and environment

23,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

Surface water discharge from the attenuation pond was sampled on Monday and sent for laboratory analysis. Results are awaited, and the pond outlet valve remains closed until they are received. Silt fences along the access road have been inspected and two sections need repair after heavy rain. Neil Morgan has asked the drainage team to clear the gully gratings before the next forecast rain.
