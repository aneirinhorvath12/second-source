## 2. Health, safety and environment

21,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

## 7. Quality

NCR 0147 was raised after the inspector found that the galvanising thickness on the handrail brackets for the compressor deck did not meet the specification. Repainting was first proposed, but the coating supplier has advised a full strip and regalvanise. Graham Sinclair has agreed the approach and the work will be booked with the galvaniser next week.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Refuelling must only take place with the spill tray in position, and the fuel delivery must be logged in the site register each time.
