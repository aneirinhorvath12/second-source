## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

52,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

## 5. Design and technical

3 RFIs open, 3 closed in the period.

A design check of the sump pump sizing for the process area showed that the calculated flow rate is slightly higher than the figure on the datasheet. The mechanical engineer has asked the vendor to confirm whether the selected pump still meets the head requirement at the higher flow. A response is expected before the datasheet is updated.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 7 submitted on 24 June 2026.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Holly Whitlock before the joint was closed up.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The overhead line crossing the site entrance has been identified as a risk to tall vehicles. Height barriers will be installed at the approach and the warning signs repositioned. Drivers of tipper trucks and mobile cranes must check the clearance before entering, and any vehicle that cannot pass safely must be turned around at the lay-by.

## 9. Any other business

Scaffold inspection tags on the tank farm bund were found to be out of date on the east elevation. The scaffolding subcontractor has been asked to re-inspect and tag the full run before the painters mobilise. Until then, the affected lifts are to be cordoned off and nobody is to work above the first lift without a written green tag.
