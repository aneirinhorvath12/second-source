**Subject:** Nesting bird checks ahead of vegetation clearance at Area 100 Inlet

Dear Bethan,

Brackmoor Engineering Ltd intends to start clearing the strip of scrub and tall grass along the eastern boundary of Area 100 Inlet on Monday 14th. Before any cutting begins, a suitably experienced ecologist will walk the strip and check for active nests, in line with the consent conditions and our method statement.

The check will cover all hedgerow, scrub and the three mature willows adjacent to the drainage ditch. Where an active nest is found, a buffer of at least 10 metres will be marked with high-visibility fencing and no work will take place inside it until the young have fledged. The ecologist will confirm in writing when each buffer can be lifted.

We have asked Orrin Commissioning to hold their mowing plant outside the buffer zones and to brief their operatives on the stop procedure. Nothing will be cut on the day of the check or the following morning unless the ecologist has signed off the area.

We would be grateful if Kieran Davies could record the check date and the ecologist's findings in the site diary, and let us know if any wildlife sightings are reported by the Hexley Chemicals estates team during the clearance period. Please contact me directly if you would like to attend the walk-round.
