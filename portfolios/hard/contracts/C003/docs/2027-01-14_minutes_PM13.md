## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

43,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Hexley Chemicals representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Positive material identification was carried out on twelve spool pieces delivered to laydown at North Kessock, Highland. Eleven matched the material certificates supplied by the vendor. One spool showed a chromium reading lower than expected and has been quarantined in the stores compound pending a retest by an independent laboratory.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

A new laydown area has been set up behind the workshop for the bulk structural steel. The hardstanding is level, drained and fenced, but the gate lock has not yet been fitted. Until it is, please ensure the chain and padlock are secured each evening and that the key is returned to the site stores before the end of the shift.

## 9. Any other business

Hard hat stickers have been issued to all supervisors this week to show their competency level. Several operatives were seen wearing old colours that no longer match the register. Supervisors should check their teams during the morning walk round and report any mismatch to the site safety adviser, who will keep the register updated.
