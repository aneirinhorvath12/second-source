**Subject:** Kessock Substation: Inspection findings on welfare facilities at North Kessock, Highland

Dear Simon,

Following the inspection of the welfare facilities at North Kessock, Highland carried out on Monday, I am writing to record the findings and to ask for the actions listed below.

The inspection covered the canteen, drying room, toilet and shower blocks, first aid room and the welfare cabins serving the night shift. Overall the facilities are in good order, but a number of items need attention before the next phase of the works begins.

**Findings:**

- The drying room extract fan is not running at the rated speed and the airflow is noticeably low. Please arrange an inspection by the mechanical contractor.
- Two of the shower heads on the upper floor are dripping and the water temperature at the outlet was above 50 degrees Celsius. The mixing valve should be checked and recorded.
- Hand wash basins in the north block have no soap dispensers filled, and the paper towel supply was empty at the time of inspection.
- The first aid room is clean and well stocked, but the eye wash station expiry date has passed. It should be replaced and the inspection log updated.
- The refuse store has bins without lids and the designated waste segregation labels are missing.
- The fire extinguisher in the canteen has an inspection tag more than a year old.

**Actions requested:** Please confirm by the end of next week that the items above have been rectified, with supporting photographs for the fan, the mixing valve and the eye wash replacement. A follow-up walk round will be carried out with Kieran Davies once the confirmation is received.

I am grateful for your attention to these matters and look forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,
