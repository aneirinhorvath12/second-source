## 2. Health, safety and environment

31,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Kieran Davies will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 5. Design and technical

7 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

Drainage falls within the bund for the diesel storage tanks were checked against the civil drawings. The gradient along the northern side appears shallower than specified, which could affect rainwater runoff toward the sump. The civil designer has been asked to confirm levels by survey and advise whether any local regrading is needed.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Commissioning certificates for the fire water pumps were checked against the test schedule. Flow and pressure results met the specified values and the controller tests were recorded. The original factory certificate was missing one page, so the vendor has been asked to send a complete copy for the handover file.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Crane access to the tank farm is restricted by the overhead cable run near the bund wall. The lifting supervisor has marked the swing radius with yellow paint and fitted a goal post to protect the cables. All lifts over this line must be planned on the lift plan and signed off by the duty engineer before the crane is moved.

## 9. Any other business

Waste segregation at the laydown yard is still not meeting the agreed standard. Timber offcuts, metal cuttings and oily rags are being placed in the same skip. A separate skip for each waste stream has been ordered and will arrive on Monday. Until then, foremen are asked to walk their gangs through the labelled bins at the start of each shift.
