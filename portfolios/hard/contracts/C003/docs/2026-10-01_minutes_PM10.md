**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

60,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Portable appliance testing on the temporary power distribution boards is now 90 per cent complete. Three extension leads failed their insulation resistance test and were removed from site. Any lead that is damaged or has missing earthing must be taken out of use immediately and reported to the electrical supervisor. Test labels must be checked before tools are issued.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 27.2%. Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 2 of 2); Area 200 Process - Structural - Erect secondary steel; Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Pour foundations (part 2 of 2); Area 100 Inlet - Piping - Fabricate spools.

## 4. Programme

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

Pipe support schedule for the tank farm was checked against the isometric drawings. Some supports listed as guides are shown as anchors on the layout, which would change the stress model. The stress and support designers agreed to align the schedule and reissue it with a clear change list for the site team.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 10 submitted on 23 September 2026.

## 7. Quality

A welder qualification record for one operative on Ashlar Scaffolding was found to have expired the previous Friday. The welder was removed from production welding immediately and the weld log was checked back against the record. Two root runs completed during the lapse have been flagged for additional ultrasonic testing.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The insulated pipe spools for Area 100 Inlet are arriving in six separate loads over the next three days. Each load needs a pre-booked slot at the goods reception cabin. Drivers must bring the delivery note, the material certificate copy and the photo ID that matches the booking. Late arrivals will be held at the weighbridge until a slot is free.

## 9. Any other business

Vehicle movement near the crane lay down has been noted as too close to the exclusion zone on several occasions. Banksmen are reminded to hold their position at the edge of the marked boundary and not to step inside it while a lift is in progress. Dedicated signage will be placed at each corner by the end of the week.
