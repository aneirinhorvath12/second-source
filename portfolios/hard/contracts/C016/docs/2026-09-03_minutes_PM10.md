## 2. Health, safety and environment

42,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 600 Control Building found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Fatima Gallagher confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

## 5. Design and technical

10 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

A query has been raised on the cathodic protection design for the buried product pipeline. The anode spacing in the calculation differs from the layout shown on the alignment sheets. The corrosion engineer is checking whether the difference reflects a later soil resistivity survey, and will confirm which version should be used for installation.

## 7. Quality

Pressure gauges used on the nitrogen purge skid were sent for recalibration to an accredited laboratory. Certificates have now been returned and checked against the instrument register. Three gauges drifted outside tolerance and have been withdrawn, with replacements fitted and recorded on the calibration log held by Craig Brennan.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Batching plant drivers must use the designated wash-out area and must not discharge into the surface water drains. The banksman will guide each mixer into position, and the site engineer will sign the delivery tickets once the slump has been checked.

Installation crews should record each seal against the relevant hatch on the inspection sheet and return the completed sheets to the site office on Friday.
