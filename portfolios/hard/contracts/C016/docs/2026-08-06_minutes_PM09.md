## 2. Health, safety and environment

19,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

Engineers suspect a fault in one of the portable reference units rather than a real leak. The unit has been removed for bench checking and a spare has been issued. Operators must still respond to every alarm as normal until the cause is confirmed.
