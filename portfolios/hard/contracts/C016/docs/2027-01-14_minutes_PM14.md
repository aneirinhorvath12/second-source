## 2. Health, safety and environment

31,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.
