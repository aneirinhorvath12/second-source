## 2. Health, safety and environment

40,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Craig Brennan will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 6 closed in the period.

Piping stress analysis for the hot oil line feeding the tank farm has been completed. Nozzle loads on the pump discharge are slightly above the vendor limits in the thermal case. Options include adding a spring hanger or relocating the adjacent anchor. The stress engineer will present both options at the design meeting on Thursday.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Nadia Thornton before the joint was closed up.
