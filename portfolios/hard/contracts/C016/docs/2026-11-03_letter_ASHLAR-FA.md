# Ashlar Scaffolding

**To:** Amira Kerr, Commercial Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**Date:** 03 November 2026

**Subject:** Scaffolding and access package: final account claim

Dear Amira,

Further to our discussions on site and the correspondence that has passed between our respective project teams over the past several months, I write to submit Ashlar Scaffolding's claim for disruption and prolongation on the scaffolding and access package at Brenfield. The claim is in the sum of £2,748,000.

**Basis of the claim**

Our programme for the package was prepared on the footing that work faces would be released to us in the order and at the times shown in the sequence Brackmoor issued at mobilisation. That has not happened. Access to a number of work faces has been given late, in some cases by several weeks, and we have been repeatedly instructed by Brackmoor's site team to move gangs from one area to another at short notice as the sequence has been revised. The effect has been that erections have been started, struck and re-erected, labour has stood waiting for areas to be cleared, and the package has run on well beyond the period we allowed for it.

Our site records, gang allocation sheets and the weekly progress returns we have submitted to Ian Ashworth's team all bear this out. We have prepared a schedule that ties each period of delay and each resequencing to the access dates actually given, and this is enclosed with the claim together with the supporting records.

**Disruption and prolongation**

The claim is made in two parts. The first covers the loss of productivity suffered while working out of sequence and in interrupted, fragmented areas. The second covers the extended period for which we have had to retain scaffold, labour, supervision and site establishment on the package. Both parts are set out in the enclosed schedule, and we would be glad to take you and Holly Ellison through the build-up of the figures at your convenience.

**Next steps**

We would much prefer to resolve this by agreement, and I am happy to meet at Brenfield or at your offices in the coming weeks. However, if the matter has not been resolved within 60 days of the date of this letter, Ashlar Scaffolding will refer it to adjudication.

Please acknowledge receipt, and let me know who on your side will be handling the claim so that we can arrange a first meeting promptly.

Yours sincerely,

Nadia Thornton
Contracts Manager
Ashlar Scaffolding
