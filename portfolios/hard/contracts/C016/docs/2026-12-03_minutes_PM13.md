## 2. Health, safety and environment

40,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Ian Ashworth will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Craig Brennan has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.
