## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

45,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Ian Ashworth will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 300 Utilities. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Tregarth Groundworks has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Craig Brennan will sample the lifting records next week.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Pipe rack widening: Priya Mensah confirmed that no instruction has been issued by the Employer's Representative and that site staff have no authority to instruct changes (clause 3.2). The Employer will not accept the cost. Brackmoor reserved its position.

## 7. Quality

Visual inspection of the stainless steel vessel internals was carried out before the manway covers were refitted. The inspector found a small amount of weld spatter on a support ring and recorded it on the inspection report. Cleaning and passivation of the affected area will be witnessed before the vessel is closed.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.

## 9. Any other business

Scaffold inspection tags on the tank farm bund were found to be out of date on the east elevation. The scaffolding subcontractor has been asked to re-inspect and tag the full run before the painters mobilise. Until then, the affected lifts are to be cordoned off and nobody is to work above the first lift without a written green tag.
