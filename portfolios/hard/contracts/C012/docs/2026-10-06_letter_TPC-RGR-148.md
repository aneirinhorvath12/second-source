This letter is issued as Employer's Representative's Instruction **ERI-027** under clause 14 of the Contract.

**Instruction**

You are instructed to enlarge the foundations for the Employer's future compressor, in accordance with the particulars below.

**Technical detail**

- The foundation block for the future compressor is to be enlarged in plan to 9.0 m × 6.5 m, with the depth increased to suit the revised loading.
- The enlarged block is to be designed for a machine operating mass of 42 tonnes. Foundation bolt positions will follow the vendor's general arrangement drawing, which we will issue under separate cover.
- Reinforcement and concrete grade are to be developed within your design, and the foundations are to remain compatible with the adjacent pipe rack and drainage arrangements.

**Quotation**

Please provide a quotation under clause 14.2 within 14 days of the date of this letter. The quotation should state any adjustment you consider necessary to the Contract Price and to the programme, with supporting build-up, and should identify any effect on your subcontractors' work.

We would also ask that the quotation sets out the date by which you would need to start the enlarged excavation, so that we can consider it alongside the current programme.
