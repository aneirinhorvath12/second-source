## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

25,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

## 4. Programme

Programme update under review following the steelwork resequencing; revised forecast to be confirmed.

## 5. Design and technical

14 RFIs open, 10 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Fit-up inspection of the pipe spools for the sea water intake was completed before welding. Bevel angles, root gaps and alignment were measured and recorded. Two joints needed minor adjustment with a hydraulic clamp, and both were rechecked by the inspector before tacking was permitted.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The temporary generator for the commissioning phase will be delivered on the low loader on Monday. It will be placed on the bunded pad at the west corner, away from the public footpath. Refuelling must only take place with the spill tray in position, and the fuel delivery must be logged in the site register each time.

## 9. Any other business

Scaffold inspection tags on the tank farm bund were found to be out of date on the east elevation. The scaffolding subcontractor has been asked to re-inspect and tag the full run before the painters mobilise. Until then, the affected lifts are to be cordoned off and nobody is to work above the first lift without a written green tag.
