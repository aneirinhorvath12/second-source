**Subject:** Dust monitoring results at the site boundary, four-week period

Dear Laura,

I write to share the results of the dust monitoring carried out at the site boundary over the last four weeks. Four continuous monitors have been operating along the boundary fence at Rothwell, West Yorkshire, one on each side of the compound, and the readings have been logged against the agreed trigger levels.

**Summary of results**

Daily mean PM10 readings stayed below the agreed trigger level on all days except one. The exception occurred on the Thursday of the second week, when excavation on the north haul road coincided with a period of dry, windy weather. The monitor on the north fence recorded a daily mean above the trigger level for that single day.

The readings for the other three monitors remained well within the limits throughout the period, and no reading on any monitor recorded a one-hour exceedance.

**Action taken**

On the day of the exceedance, the haul road was watered by a bowser every hour and the excavation was paused during the strongest gusts. A wind-speed alarm has since been added to the excavation procedure, so that work stops automatically when the on-site anemometer reads above the set limit. The water bowser schedule has been increased for dry weather, and the haul road surface is being treated with a dust suppressant from the coming week.

The monitoring data, the calibration certificates and the wind records for the period are enclosed. Ravi Kowalski will continue to review the readings each week and will alert you directly if any trigger level is approached.

Please let me know if you would like the monitoring frequency increased for the next phase of excavation works.
