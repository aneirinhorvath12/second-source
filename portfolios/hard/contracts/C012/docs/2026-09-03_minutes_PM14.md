## 2. Health, safety and environment

29,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 500 Export found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Holly Patel confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Ravi Kowalski will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.
