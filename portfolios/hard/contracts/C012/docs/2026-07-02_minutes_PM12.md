## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 300 Utilities. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Marlow Insulation has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Isla Kerr will sample the lifting records next week.

Surface water discharge from the attenuation pond was sampled on Monday and sent for laboratory analysis. Results are awaited, and the pond outlet valve remains closed until they are received. Silt fences along the access road have been inspected and two sections need repair after heavy rain. Ravi Kowalski has asked the drainage team to clear the gully gratings before the next forecast rain.

## 5. Design and technical

11 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

The hydrostatic test package for the sour water line in Area 200 Process was witnessed by Neil Thornton and Daniel Quigley on Tuesday. Test pressure was held at 1.5 times design for thirty minutes with no loss recorded on the gauge. The test certificate has been signed by both parties and the line is ready for reinstatement of the flanges.
