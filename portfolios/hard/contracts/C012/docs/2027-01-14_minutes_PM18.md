## 2. Health, safety and environment

58,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Daniel Quigley will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Tamsin Petrochemicals representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

The commissioning team raised a query about the vent line sizing on the methanol storage tank. The current line appears undersized for the maximum breathing load in the event of rapid filling. The process engineer is checking the calculation and will confirm whether the line size or the filling rate should change.

## 7. Quality

Dimensional checks on the skid frame for the chemical dosing package were recorded against the approved fabrication drawings. Base plate levels were within tolerance, but the nozzle orientation on one vessel was rotated by about four degrees. The fabricator has been asked to submit a proposed correction for review by the design team.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.
