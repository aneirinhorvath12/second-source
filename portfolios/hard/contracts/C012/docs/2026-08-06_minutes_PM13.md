## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

32,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

The LOLER thorough examination of the 25 tonne mobile crane is due next Thursday, and the current certificate was checked at the meeting. The hook block and wire rope inspection records are complete, but the outrigger pad condition report is still outstanding from the hire company. Until it is received, the crane will only be used within the limits of the existing load chart, and lifts will be planned accordingly.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

## 3. Progress

## 4. Programme

## 5. Design and technical

7 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

A technical query from Marlow Insulation concerns the material specification for the gaskets on the sulphuric acid line. The drawing calls for a PTFE-based gasket, but the purchase specification lists a graphite option. Engineering has been asked to confirm the chemical compatibility and to issue a single approved specification to both teams.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Document review of the as-built drawings for the water treatment plant continued this week. Several isometrics showed valves in the wrong position, and the markups have been passed to the drawing office. A revised set will be issued once the changes have been checked against the latest site photographs and the field notes.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The temporary power supply to the welfare compound will be switched over on Monday afternoon. The distribution board was tested by the electrician last Friday and the earth continuity readings were recorded. Anyone working on the site office kettles, heaters or chargers should unplug them before the changeover at fourteen hundred hours.

Daniel Quigley will confirm the format at the next meeting so that the record can be filed correctly.
