# Hexley Chemicals

**To:** Kieran Hale, Commercial Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**Date:** 09 October 2026

**Subject:** Fennick Hydrogen Pilot: your variation VO-002

Dear Kieran,

This letter concerns the Fennick Hydrogen Pilot contract only. I am writing as Employer's Representative for Hexley Chemicals in response to your submission of variation VO-002, which proposes a change to the contract sum of £780,373 for the replacement of the security fencing around the pilot plant compound.

I have reviewed your submission against the Employer's Requirements and the drawings and specification that formed part of the contract documents at signature. The perimeter security fencing, including the removal of the existing fence line and its replacement to the standard described in the Requirements, is part of the scope Brackmoor Engineering Ltd agreed to carry out under the contract. It is not an addition to, or change from, that scope. The works are therefore within the original scope of the contract, and I do not regard them as a variation.

VO-002 is accordingly **not accepted**. No adjustment will be made to the contract sum or to the programme on the basis of that submission, and you should not treat this letter, or any earlier discussion on site about the condition of the existing fence, as an instruction to carry out additional work.

I note that your submission refers to the condition of the existing fence line as found. If you consider that you have encountered something that was not reasonably foreseeable from the information provided to you at tender, you are free to set that out separately, with supporting records, and it will be considered on its own merits. It would need to be a distinct submission and not a revision of VO-002.

For good order, please would you ensure that the fencing works continue in line with your current programme. Our Site Representative, who has been copied into this letter, will be in touch with your site team about access arrangements for the compound gate during the changeover, as the pilot plant commissioning team will need to maintain controlled entry throughout.

Please acknowledge receipt of this letter by return. I am happy to discuss it if that would be helpful, though my position on VO-002 is as stated above.

Yours sincerely,

Lucy Okafor
Employer's Representative
