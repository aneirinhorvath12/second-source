## 2. Health, safety and environment

47,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

A spill response drill in the tank farm simulated a diesel release from a damaged transfer hose. The team deployed booms within eleven minutes, and the drain valves were closed as planned. Some confusion arose over who should contact the environment officer, so the call-out list at the bund gate will be updated. A debrief with all shift leaders is scheduled for Friday morning.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Hallam Steel Erectors will provide feedback at the next meeting.
