## 2. Health, safety and environment

43,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Owen Morgan has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Ian Brennan will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Daniel Davies so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Heavy rain over the weekend left standing water in the pipe rack corridor and on the access road beyond the second gate. Plant operators have been asked to check the surface before driving heavy equipment across. Temporary sump pumps will be relocated to the lowest point by the drainage contractor, and the site team will report any further flooding to the weekly safety forum.
