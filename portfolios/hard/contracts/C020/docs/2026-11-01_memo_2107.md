# Internal memo

**To:** Daniel Davies, Site Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Ian Brennan, Clerk of Works
**Date:** 01 November 2026
**Subject:** NCR close-out: weld repair documentation on the Area 300 Utilities pipework

This memo records the position on the non-conformance report raised against the repair of a rejected circumferential weld on the 150 mm process line in Area 600 Control Building.

**Status:** Repair complete and re-inspected. Documentation is incomplete and the NCR cannot be closed until the items below are in the file.

**Outstanding items:**

- The weld repair procedure reference has been entered on the repair record but the welder's qualification certificate for the repair operative is not attached. The certificate must show the process and position covered by the repair.
- The post-repair radiographic film has been reviewed and accepted, but the film identification number on the test report does not match the film log. Please ask the NDT subcontractor to reissue the report with the correct reference.
- The heat treatment chart is missing the thermocouple layout sketch. Without it the chart cannot be verified against the procedure.
- The grinding depth and the re-weld dimensions are recorded in millimetres on one sheet and in inches on another. One format should be used throughout.
- The hardness survey results are satisfactory but the report has not been countersigned by the authorised inspector.

**Actions:** Please have the welder certificate and the reissued radiographic report sent to the document controller by Thursday. I will walk the line again with the inspector once the heat treatment sketch and hardness report are in place.

Once all five items have been confirmed, I will sign the NCR for close-out and the record will be passed to the customer's clerk of works for their review.
