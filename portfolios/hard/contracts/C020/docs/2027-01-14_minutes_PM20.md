## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

36,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 200 Process. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Ridgeway Civils has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Lucy Walsh will sample the lifting records next week.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Ian Brennan will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.

## 5. Design and technical

11 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

The commissioning team raised a query about the vent line sizing on the methanol storage tank. The current line appears undersized for the maximum breathing load in the event of rapid filling. The process engineer is checking the calculation and will confirm whether the line size or the filling rate should change.

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 200 Process identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

Please fill any hand-wash stations and check the site toilet cisterns before the shutdown begins, as the supply will be off.
