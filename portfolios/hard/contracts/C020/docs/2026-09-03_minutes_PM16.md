## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

20,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

Segregated walkways and vehicle routes have been reviewed around the new tanker bay. Delivery drivers were seen reversing without a banksman on two occasions last week. A second banksman will be rostered during peak delivery hours, and yellow lines have been repainted at the turning circle. Gareth Sinclair has been notified of the change to the logistics route through the customer gate.

## 5. Design and technical

8 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 7. Quality

Cable tray earthing continuity tests were completed in the process area at Weybury, Hampshire. Results were logged for each tray section and any readings above the agreed limit were investigated. A loose bonding clamp was found and tightened, and the section was retested with a satisfactory result recorded on the form.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Daniel Davies so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Replacement mats are due on site next Tuesday. Lucy Walsh asked that the lift plan be reissued once the new mats are in place, with the ground bearing calculation attached for the lifting supervisor's sign-off.
