# Weybury Effluent Plant: progress meeting no. 14

**Date:** 02 July 2026, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Rhys Lowther (ER), Gareth Sinclair, Ian Brennan, Neil Lindqvist (Sorrel Renewables); Lucy Walsh, Isla Hale, Daniel Davies, Holly Gallagher (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** Daniel Davies

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

29,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Ian Brennan will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 38.7%. Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Backfill and compact (part 3 of 3); Area 100 Inlet - Piping - Install spools; Area 200 Process - Civils - Excavate and blind (part 1 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Structural - Install grating and handrail.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 29 December 2027 against contract completion 18 June 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

5 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

Structural steel connection details for the pipe rack in Area 600 Control Building were queried by the erection team. Several gusset plates appear to conflict with the revised beam sizes shown in the latest general arrangement. The structural engineer has agreed to reissue the connection schedule, and erectors will hold work on those bays until the new details are confirmed.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 14 submitted on 27 June 2026.

## 7. Quality

Calibration of the flow meters on the custody transfer line was witnessed by Rhys Lowther. The meters were checked against a reference prover and the results were within the permitted error band. Certificates have been filed and the meter tag numbers matched to the instrument index.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The overhead line crossing the site entrance has been identified as a risk to tall vehicles. Height barriers will be installed at the approach and the warning signs repositioned. Drivers of tipper trucks and mobile cranes must check the clearance before entering, and any vehicle that cannot pass safely must be turned around at the lay-by.

## 9. Any other business

Lunchtime access to the pipe rack walkway has been restricted because of the scaffold modification underway. Operatives are reminded that the walkway is closed and that the alternative route via the service stair must be used. Any person found on the walkway during the closure will be removed from the site for the rest of the day.
