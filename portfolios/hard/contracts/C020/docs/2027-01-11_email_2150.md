I have reviewed the dust monitoring results for the four monitoring points on the site boundary for the last four weeks. The summary is below for information.

All readings were within the trigger levels set in the dust management plan. The highest 24-hour average at any point was recorded at the south gate monitor, on the day the concrete breaking started. The reading was below the action level, but it was higher than the other points and is worth keeping an eye on while that activity continues.

The north and east monitors showed low readings throughout, which is consistent with the wind direction recorded on site for most of the period. The west monitor was out of service for two days because of a power interruption. The data logger has been reset and readings have resumed.

The following points are recommended:

- Dampen the haul road at the south gate before concrete breaking starts each morning.
- Keep the wheel wash running during the first hour of each shift, when vehicle movements are highest.
- Record the wind speed and direction against each breaking activity so that any future exceedance can be traced more easily.
