**Subject:** Amber weather warning and high-wind lifting restrictions

The Met Office has issued an amber weather warning for the site area from 14:00 tomorrow until 06:00 on Thursday. Your crews must apply the following restrictions from the moment the warning takes effect.

**Lifting operations**

- All crane lifts must stop when the site anemometer on the north gantry reads a sustained wind speed of 20 mph or gusts above 30 mph.
- Lifts that are already under way must be lowered to a safe position and secured before the crew leaves the lift.
- Loads with a large sail area, such as cladding panels or sheet material, must not be lifted at any wind speed above 15 mph.
- Banksmen must check the wind readings on the display at the start of every lift and log them on the permit.

**Work at height**

- Work on scaffolding, cherry pickers and steel erection above 10 metres must stop when the wind exceeds 25 mph.
- All scaffold tags should be checked before the warning starts, and any loose boards or ties reported to Lucy Walsh.

**Housekeeping**

- Secure all loose materials, sheeting, and temporary signs before 14:00.
- Empty skips and store small tools in lockable containers.
- Check that the tower lights and barriers around Area 200 Process are still in place.

**Monitoring**

- The site team will check the forecast at 06:00 and again at 12:00 each day while the warning is active.
- Your supervisor must confirm to me by text when your crew has stopped and secured the work.

Please brief your operatives at the morning meeting tomorrow and sign the sheet to confirm that they have understood these restrictions.
