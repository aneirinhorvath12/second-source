# Merrow Bay Terminal Expansion: progress meeting no. 13

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Claire Ferris (ER), Craig Morgan, Holly Carver, Sophie Lowther (Norvale Energy plc); Aisha Yates, Grace Brennan, Ade Gallagher, Dev Quigley (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

40,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 46.8%. Activities progressed this period: Area 200 Process - Mechanical - Align rotating equipment; Area 300 Utilities - Structural - Install grating and handrail; Area 100 Inlet - Mechanical - Install package skids; Area 400 Storage - Structural - Erect secondary steel.

Productivity remains below plan; new supervision team mobilised.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 19 March 2028 against contract completion 25 July 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

8 RFIs open, 3 closed in the period.

A design check of the sump pump sizing for the process area showed that the calculated flow rate is slightly higher than the figure on the datasheet. The mechanical engineer has asked the vendor to confirm whether the selected pump still meets the head requirement at the higher flow. A response is expected before the datasheet is updated.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 13 submitted on 10 January 2027.

BRK/MBT/Q/052: valuation still not agreed; the parties will meet in February to try to close it out.

## 7. Quality

Weekly quality meeting minutes were circulated to the site team and the customer representatives. Key points included a rise in minor NCRs relating to paint touch-ups, a request for better labelling of spool pieces, and confirmation that the calibration register will be audited next month by Claire Ferris.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The temporary generator for the commissioning phase will be delivered on the low loader on Monday. It will be placed on the bunded pad at the west corner, away from the public footpath. Refuelling must only take place with the spill tray in position, and the fuel delivery must be logged in the site register each time.

## 9. Any other business

Nitrogen purge bottles used for the pipework closures have been left uncapped in several places. The cylinder store team has been asked to chain all bottles to the rack at the end of each shift. Any cylinder found standing unsecured will be returned to the supplier and replaced on the next delivery.
