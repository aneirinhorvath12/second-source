# Merrow Bay Terminal Expansion: progress meeting no. 11

**Date:** 05 November 2026, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Claire Ferris (ER), Craig Morgan, Holly Carver, Sophie Lowther (Norvale Energy plc); Aisha Yates, Grace Brennan, Ade Gallagher, Dev Quigley (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** Ade Gallagher

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

55,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

Surface water discharge from the attenuation pond was sampled on Monday and sent for laboratory analysis. Results are awaited, and the pond outlet valve remains closed until they are received. Silt fences along the access road have been inspected and two sections need repair after heavy rain. Ade Gallagher has asked the drainage team to clear the gully gratings before the next forecast rain.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 38.6%. Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Excavate and blind (part 2 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Backfill and compact (part 2 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 3 of 3); Area 200 Process - Civils - Pour foundations (part 3 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 19 March 2028 against contract completion 25 July 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

12 RFIs open, 3 closed in the period.

Pipe support schedule for the tank farm was checked against the isometric drawings. Some supports listed as guides are shown as anchors on the layout, which would change the stress model. The stress and support designers agreed to align the schedule and reissue it with a clear change list for the site team.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 11 submitted on 28 October 2026.

BRK/MBT/Q/047 (temporary power extension): ER reviewing; response awaited.

## 7. Quality

Material traceability for the carbon steel flanges was reviewed in the stores office. Heat numbers on several boxes were partly illegible due to weather exposure. The storekeeper transferred the information from the mill certificates, marked the boxes again and added a note to the stock record.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Dev Quigley first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

The north access gate at Merrow Bay, Pembrokeshire will be closed on Thursday from 14:00 to 17:00 for the drainage manhole tie-in. Insulation deliveries should use the south gate instead. Ade Gallagher is to confirm that the traffic marshal is booked and that the revised plan is posted at the security hut before the weekend shift. Drivers arriving outside the booked windows will be held at the barrier until the marshal is available.
