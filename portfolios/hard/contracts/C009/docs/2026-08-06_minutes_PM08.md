## 2. Health, safety and environment

50,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Aisha Yates will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Holly Carver will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.
