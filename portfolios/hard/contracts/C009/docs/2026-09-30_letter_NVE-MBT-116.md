This letter is issued under clause 14 of the Contract as Employer's Representative's Instruction **ERI-019**.

**Instruction**

You are instructed to carry out additional earthing and lightning protection to the new standard, in accordance with the revised requirements now adopted by Norvale Energy plc for the Terminal.

**Technical detail**

- Earthing: a supplementary buried copper ring main is to be installed around the new process area, with bonded connections to the structural steelwork columns at a maximum spacing of 20 m.
- Lightning protection: air termination and down-conductor arrangements are to be extended to the new pipe rack and the associated equipment enclosures, with test points at ground level on each down conductor.
- Testing: earth resistance values and continuity are to be tested and recorded for the complete system, and the results provided to my office.
