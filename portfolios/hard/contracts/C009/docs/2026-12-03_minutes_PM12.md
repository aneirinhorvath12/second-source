## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

30,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Hallam Steel Erectors will provide feedback at the next meeting.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

Activities progressed this period: Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Pour foundations (part 2 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Structural - Erect secondary steel; Area 200 Process - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 2 of 3); Area 400 Storage - Civils - Pour foundations (part 3 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 19 March 2028 against contract completion 25 July 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

A model clash review covering the chemical dosing area highlighted interference between a cable tray and a new sample line. The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

Quality records for the pipe supports on Area 500 Export were audited against the drawing register. Several support drawings had been superseded without the earlier revision being withdrawn from site. The document controller has issued a list of affected drawings and the site team has been asked to remove obsolete copies from the work packs.

Anyone working on the site office kettles, heaters or chargers should unplug them before the changeover at fourteen hundred hours.
