## 2. Health, safety and environment

33,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

## 5. Design and technical

12 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

Equipment layout drawings for the new skid-mounted filtration units were marked up by the commissioning team. Their comments focus on isolation valve positions and drain points that are hard to reach. The layout designer will review the markups and reissue the drawing with revised access arrangements shown clearly for operators.

## 7. Quality

Non-destructive testing reports for the pressure vessel nozzles were issued by the independent inspection body. Magnetic particle testing found no relevant indications on the attachment welds. The reports have been checked against the weld map and signed off by Holly Carver, so the vessel can move to the next stage.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The site welfare cabins are being connected to the mains water supply on Wednesday. The plumber will need access to the cabin roofs and the rear service duct. Please move any personal items away from the cabin walls and ensure the taps are closed when you leave, so the pressure test can be carried out without interruption.

## 9. Any other business

Some of the steel stored on sleepers at the east end of the yard has started to show surface rust after the wet spell. The material controller has been asked to check the certificates and mill markings before the items are moved into the covered store. Any material with heavy corrosion will be set aside for inspection.
