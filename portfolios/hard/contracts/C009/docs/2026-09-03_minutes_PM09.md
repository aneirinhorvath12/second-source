## 2. Health, safety and environment

25,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Ade Gallagher will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.
