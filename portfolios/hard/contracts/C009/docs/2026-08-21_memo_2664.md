# Internal memo

**To:** Aisha Yates, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Ade Gallagher, Site Manager
**Date:** 21 August 2026
**Subject:** Permit-to-work audit results, Area 200 Process, week 22

## Summary

The weekly permit-to-work audit for Area 400 Storage was carried out on Tuesday with Holly Carver in attendance. Eighteen live permits were sampled across hot work, confined space and lifting operations. Overall compliance was good, with no permits found in use outside their validity window.

## Findings

- **Hot work:** Two fire watch logs were completed after the task ended but not at the time of completion. Both supervisors have been reminded.
- **Isolations:** One lock-off certificate had the wrong tag reference. The correct tag was confirmed on site and the record has been amended.
- **Gas testing:** All gas test readings were recorded at the required intervals and signed.
- **Housekeeping:** Two work areas beside the pipe rack had trip hazards from unused scaffold tube. These were cleared before the end of the shift.

## Actions

1. Permit issuers to complete fire watch entries at the point the task finishes, not at the end of the shift.
2. Isolation tags to be checked against the permit register at the morning briefing.
3. Area supervisors to walk their work front at 14:00 each day to check for loose materials.

The next audit is scheduled for the same day next week. Please let me know if you would like the full sample list for your records.
