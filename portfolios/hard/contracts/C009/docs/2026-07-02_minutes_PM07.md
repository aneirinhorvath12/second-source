## 2. Health, safety and environment

57,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Holly Carver will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Daniel Ashworth has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.
