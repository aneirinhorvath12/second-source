Dear Aisha,

Thank you for your quotation BRK/MBT/Q/043. I have reviewed it against the Employer's Requirements and the design submissions made to date, and set out my response below.

The quotation describes additional stiffening to the pipe rack steelwork and a revised arrangement of pipe supports at the northern end of the rack. As I understand it, these changes follow from the detailed stress analysis of the process piping, which showed that the support spacing and guide locations in your earlier layout would not keep line loads and deflections within the limits set by the Employer's Requirements. The Employer's Requirements set those limits, and they have not changed since the Contract was signed. The piping arrangement, the support philosophy and the rack framing were all developed by Brackmoor and its designers, and the analysis results are the normal product of that development. Nothing in them points to a change in the performance required, the design basis or the scope the Employer asked for.

The same applies to the revised support details. Where a design has to be developed, and its supports and secondary steelwork adjusted, so that it satisfies criteria already stated in the Employer's Requirements, that is design development. It is not a change to those requirements.

My conclusion is therefore that this work arises from the development of the Contractor's design to meet the Employer's Requirements. Under clause 2.3 it forms part of the Contractor's obligations under the Contract, and no adjustment to the Contract Price is due in respect of it.

The Employer has no objection to the revised support arrangement proceeding, subject to the usual design review. Please submit the updated calculations and the revised rack and support drawings through the normal document control route, so that Craig Morgan's team can complete the review before any fabrication drawings are released. I would also be grateful if Ade Gallagher could confirm with Hallam Steel Erectors how the revised steelwork fits with their current erection sequence.

I am happy to discuss any of this with you and Grace Brennan if that would help.

Yours sincerely,
