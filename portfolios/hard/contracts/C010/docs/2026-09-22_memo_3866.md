# Internal memo

**To:** Simon Davies, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Zoe Strachan, Site Manager
**Date:** 22 September 2026
**Subject:** Near-miss report: dropped timber board from north face scaffold, Area 400 Storage

At approximately 10:40 on Tuesday, a 300 mm length of timber board slipped from the materials hoist on the north face scaffold at level 3 of Area 400 Storage. It fell about eight metres and landed on the exclusion barrier at ground level. Nobody was in the exclusion zone at the time and nobody was struck.

**Immediate actions**

- The exclusion zone was extended to cover the full scaffold footprint and remained in place until the bay was inspected.
- The scaffold tagging supervisor inspected the hoist and the adjacent bays. A toe board was found missing on the bay below, which is now reinstated.
- The hoist was taken out of use for the rest of the shift and returned after the scaffold inspector signed the release.

**Likely cause**

The board had been loaded on top of two other boards and was not strapped. The gang was working from a loading point that had been agreed for the earlier phase, not the current one.

**Actions for the coming week**

- Toolbox talk for all scaffold and materials handling gangs on securing loads and the hoist loading points, to be held before the next lift.
- Hoist loading point signage to be updated on each level.
- Weekly scaffold inspection to include a check of the hoist area and loading points.

Please confirm you are content with these actions so the report can be closed on the incident log.
