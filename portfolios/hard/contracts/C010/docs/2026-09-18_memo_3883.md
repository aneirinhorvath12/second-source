# Internal memo

**To:** Simon Davies, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Nadia Fraser, Planning Manager
**Date:** 18 September 2026
**Subject:** Lightning protection inspection, flare stack area, Area 200 Process

This memo confirms the revised programme for the lightning protection inspection on the flare stack and associated air terminals within Area 200 Process. The inspection was previously shown in week 22 and has moved to week 24 to suit the availability of the testing engineer.

**Scope of inspection**

- continuity testing of the down conductors from each air terminal to the earth pit
- measurement of earth electrode resistance at all four pits, with results against the design value of 10 ohms or less
- visual inspection of bonding to the handrails, ladders and cable trays
- check of the test joints and their labelling

**Access requirements**

The stack will need to be isolated from the vent system for the duration of the work on the upper section. The operations team has been asked to confirm the isolation window, and the permit will be raised once that is agreed. Scaffold access for the upper section is booked for the Tuesday and Wednesday of week 24.
