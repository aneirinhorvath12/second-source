# Internal memo

**To:** Simon Davies, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Zoe Strachan, Site Manager
**Date:** 19 December 2026
**Subject:** Near-miss report: dropped scaffold clip, Area 100 Inlet level 2

**Date and time:** Tuesday, 07:40, during the morning toolbox start.

**What happened:** A scaffold tie clip fell from the working lift on the north face of Area 200 Process at level 2. It landed on the hardstanding approximately 3 metres from a welder who was setting up his equipment. Nobody was struck.

**Immediate actions taken:**

- Exclusion zone extended to 6 metres around the north elevation.
- Scaffold inspected by the scaffold sub-contractor's tagged inspector before work resumed at 10:15.
- Loose tools and fittings on the affected lift cleared and retained as evidence.
- Toe boards and mesh checked along the full lift length.

**Likely cause:** The tie clip was not secured to the lift board after the previous shift's handover. The lift had been partly stripped and left unattended overnight.

**Proposed actions:**

1. Reminder at tomorrow's toolbox talk that all loose fittings must be bagged and tagged before leaving a lift.
2. Scaffold handover checklist to be signed by both outgoing and incoming supervisors.
3. Daily walk-down of the north face by the scaffold supervisor for the next two weeks.

Please confirm you are content with these actions so I can update the near-miss log today.
