**Subject:** Crane lift plan review, heat exchanger shell lift

Following the review meeting on Monday, please find below the points raised on the draft lift plan for the heat exchanger shell lift on Area 300 Utilities. The lift is to be made with our 350 tonne crawler crane and the shell weighs approximately 42 tonnes including rigging.

**Points to close before the lift**

- Ground bearing: the bearing capacity of the crane pad needs to be confirmed on the ground investigation report. Our engineer has asked for the mat specification to be checked against the outrigger loads shown on page 6.
- Exclusion zone: the zone shown on the drawing does not include the pipe rack access route. This must be extended and marked on site before the crane is erected.
- Slinging arrangement: the revised sling angles give a lower safety factor on the chokers than the original plan. We have proposed a spreader beam, which is shown in the attached sketch.
- Wind limits: the lift will stop if the wind exceeds 10 metres per second at the boom head. A wind gauge should be mounted on the crane at the point of the lift and read by the lift supervisor.
- Tag lines: two tag lines are required on the shell. The banksmen should be identified by name on the permit before the day of the lift.

**Documents needed**

Please send the load test certificate for the spreader beam and the latest inspection report for the crane. We also need confirmation that the lift supervisor and the appointed person will both be on site for the full duration.

We can be ready to make the lift on the planned date if these points are closed by Thursday. Please call me if any of the items need discussing before then.
