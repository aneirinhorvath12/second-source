## 2. Health, safety and environment

40,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Rhys Hartley will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.
