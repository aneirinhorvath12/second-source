Dear Simon,

Thank you for your quotation BRK/GPS/Q/038. I have reviewed it with Ravi Brennan and Euan Hale, and I have also looked again at the relevant design submissions and the Employer's Requirements.

The quotation presents the revised arrangement at the Glenwick spur as a change to the scope. Our reading is different. The Employer's Requirements set the performance criteria for the line: the operating envelope, the allowable movement at the tie-in, and the thermal and drainage performance of the installation. They do not prescribe pipe support spacing, anchor locations or the detailing of the insulation and cladding interfaces. Those were left to Brackmoor to develop.

The design you submitted at the outset assumed a support and guide arrangement that, once the thermal expansion calculations were completed in detail, did not keep the line within the allowable movement at the tie-in. The additional guide frames and the associated adjustments to the insulation details follow directly from that calculation. They are the means by which your own design is brought into compliance with the stated criteria. The same applies to the local changes to the drainage falls, which result from the revised support levels and not from any change to the criteria we set.

In short, the work described in the quotation arises from developing Brackmoor's design to meet the Employer's Requirements. Under clause 2.3 it forms part of the Contractor's obligations, and no adjustment to the Contract Price is due. I have not issued an instruction for this work, and none is intended.

I appreciate that the team has put effort into pricing the arrangement, and I am happy for Fatima Talbot to go through our reasoning with Euan if that would help. Nothing in this letter should hold up the work itself. We would expect the revised details to proceed through the normal design submission process so that the programme is not affected.
