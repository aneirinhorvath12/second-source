## 2. Health, safety and environment

23,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 200 Process. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Marlow Insulation has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Simon Davies will sample the lifting records next week.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.
