## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

32,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

The scaffold on the south side of the separator vessel was inspected and tagged green following the recent modifications. The inspector noted that a toe board was missing on one lift and a ladder access was positioned incorrectly. Both points were corrected before the tag was signed. Users should check the tag before access and report any damage or loose fittings immediately to the scaffold foreman.

## 7. Quality

Document review of the as-built drawings for the water treatment plant continued this week. The customer's plant manager has requested that the flare header tie-in is planned for a night shift when the adjacent unit is in standby. Tom Whitlock will attend the pre-start meeting to confirm the isolation points. Lighting towers are to be positioned along the access road to give safe walking routes to the tie-in location.

## 9. Any other business

A reminder that all hot work permits must be countersigned by the area authority before the grinder or welding set is brought into Area 300 Utilities. Several permits last week were found to be missing the gas test record. Permit holders should bring their own gas detector to the morning briefing, and fire watch cover must continue for thirty minutes after the final task has finished.
