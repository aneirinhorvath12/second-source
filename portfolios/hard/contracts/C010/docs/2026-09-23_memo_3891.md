**Subject:** Snagging walk-down arrangements for Area 500 Export mechanical packages

This memo sets out how the internal snagging walk-downs for your mechanical packages in Area 500 Export will run over the next three weeks.

**Schedule**

- Week 1: Pump skids P-201 to P-206, walk-down on Monday and Tuesday
- Week 2: Heat exchangers E-301 and E-302, walk-down on Wednesday
- Week 3: Packaged compressor unit and associated pipework, walk-down on Thursday

Each walk-down starts at 14:00 and should last no more than two hours. Your foreman and one of your supervisors should attend, along with a member of our commissioning team.

**Before each walk-down**

- Clear the area of loose materials and temporary tools.
- Make sure access platforms are in place and scaffold tags are current.
- Have the relevant datasheets and inspection test plans available on a tablet or in paper form.

**During the walk-down**

Any item found will be recorded on the snagging list with a reference number, photograph and priority. Priority 1 items must be closed before the system is handed to commissioning. Priority 2 and 3 items should be closed within five working days of the walk-down.

Please send me the name of your nominated attendee for each session by Friday, so that the access permits can be prepared in advance.
