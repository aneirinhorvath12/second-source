## 2. Health, safety and environment

57,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Zoe Strachan will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

A reversing dumper almost struck a stored gas bottle rack at the laydown yard when its reversing alarm was found to be disconnected. The operator stopped when a banksman signalled, and no contact was made. The machine has been removed from service pending repair. All plant operators have been reminded that alarms and lights must work and that pre-start checks must be recorded in the logbook.

## 5. Design and technical

10 RFIs open, 4 closed in the period.

A model clash review covering the chemical dosing area highlighted interference between a cable tray and a new sample line. The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

Pressure gauges used on the nitrogen purge skid were sent for recalibration to an accredited laboratory. Certificates have now been returned and checked against the instrument register. Three gauges drifted outside tolerance and have been withdrawn, with replacements fitted and recorded on the calibration log held by Simon Davies.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Two pallets of valves were found blocking the hydrant on Tuesday, and the fire officer has asked for the area to be signed. Please keep the route to the hydrant open so that a fire tender can reach it within two minutes of an alarm.
