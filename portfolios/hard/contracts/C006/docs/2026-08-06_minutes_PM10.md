## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Eastmarch Utilities representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Orrin Commissioning will provide feedback at the next meeting.

## 5. Design and technical

7 RFIs open, 10 closed in the period.

Instrument datasheets for the flow transmitters on the gas compression package contain inconsistent process conditions. Two sheets show a line pressure of 42 barg, while the heat and mass balance uses 38 barg. The instrumentation engineer has raised an RFI to confirm the operating case and will update the affected datasheets once the process team responds.

## 7. Quality

Positive material identification was carried out on twelve spool pieces delivered to laydown at Fennick, Cheshire. Eleven matched the material certificates supplied by the vendor. One spool showed a chromium reading lower than expected and has been quarantined in the stores compound pending a retest by an independent laboratory.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Zoe Kerr first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

Crane mats for the lift on the compressor skid were found cracked at the edges during Monday's inspection. The damaged mats have been removed from the laydown area and tagged. Replacement mats are due on site next Tuesday. Tom Quigley asked that the lift plan be reissued once the new mats are in place, with the ground bearing calculation attached for the lifting supervisor's sign-off.
