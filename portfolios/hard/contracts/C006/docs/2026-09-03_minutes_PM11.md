## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Segregated walkways and vehicle routes have been reviewed around the new tanker bay. Delivery drivers were seen reversing without a banksman on two occasions last week. A second banksman will be rostered during peak delivery hours, and yellow lines have been repainted at the turning circle. Lucy Talbot has been notified of the change to the logistics route through the customer gate.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Orrin Commissioning will provide feedback at the next meeting.

## 7. Quality

The hydrostatic test package for the sour water line in Area 600 Control Building was witnessed by Graham Lowther and Craig Yates on Tuesday. Test pressure was held at 1.5 times design for thirty minutes with no loss recorded on the gauge. The test certificate has been signed by both parties and the line is ready for reinstatement of the flanges.
