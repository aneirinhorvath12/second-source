# Fennick Hydrogen Pilot: progress meeting no. 15

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Graham Lowther (ER), Lucy Talbot, Craig Yates, Bethan Bryant (Eastmarch Utilities); Tom Quigley, Claire Morgan, James Mensah, Zoe Kerr (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

31,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Marcus Ellison has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

Harness inspection records for Orrin Commissioning show that eleven fall arrest harnesses were removed from service during the month because of frayed stitching or damaged buckles. Replacements have been issued and the old harnesses have been cut up and disposed of. Each worker must present their harness for a pre-use check before going onto the pipe rack, and supervisors will log the checks on the daily permit.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 66.6%. Activities progressed this period: Area 500 Export - Piping - Hydrotest; Area 600 Control Building - Mechanical - Install package skids; Area 200 Process - Electrical - Terminate and test; Area 400 Storage - Electrical - Pull cables (part 1 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 11 August 2027 against contract completion 29 November 2027.

## 5. Design and technical

3 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

Model review of the compressor building was carried out with Graham Lowther and Craig Yates present. Walkways and access routes were tested against the maintenance envelope requirements for the reciprocating machine. Two platforms were found to restrict lifting access, and the design team will propose revised steelwork to restore clear routes.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 15 submitted on 08 January 2027.

## 7. Quality

The coating holiday test on the buried pipeline section was carried out at the trench bottom before backfill. Four small defects were found and repaired with the approved patch material. Each repair was retested at the specified voltage and the results were entered on the holiday log for Lucy Talbot to review.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Fire hydrant access on the east side of the unit must stay clear at all times. Two pallets of valves were found blocking the hydrant on Tuesday, and the fire officer has asked for the area to be signed. Please keep the route to the hydrant open so that a fire tender can reach it within two minutes of an alarm.

## 9. Any other business

Crane mats for the lift on the compressor skid were found cracked at the edges during Monday's inspection. The damaged mats have been removed from the laydown area and tagged. Replacement mats are due on site next Tuesday. Tom Quigley asked that the lift plan be reissued once the new mats are in place, with the ground bearing calculation attached for the lifting supervisor's sign-off.
