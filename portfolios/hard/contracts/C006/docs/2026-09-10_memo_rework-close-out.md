**From:** Tom Quigley, Project Manager
**Date:** 10 September 2026
**Subject:** Pipe rack rework: close-out

Claire, Gareth,

For the record, the pipe rack rework caused by the supplier's defective steel is complete and **closed out**. The affected members have been replaced and the rack is back in the normal construction sequence. Nothing remains open on site from this episode, and I have asked James Mensah to confirm that the associated site records have been filed accordingly.

I know the rework took up a good deal of attention over the summer, so I want to be clear about where we are now. Productivity on the remaining work has been at or above plan since the rework finished. Zoe Kerr has been tracking this against the programme, and I have seen nothing in the recent progress figures to suggest any lingering effect on the crews or on the follow-on activities. I will flag it at once if that changes, but at present I don't expect it to.

On the supplier, they have **accepted liability** for the defective steel. Brackmoor will pursue recovery separately from the project. That is a matter between us and the supplier, and it should be kept apart from the project's day-to-day reporting. Recovery is not in the forecast, and I'd ask that nobody builds it into any figures or projections until it is actually resolved.

Gareth, please keep the supplier correspondence and the rework records together in one place so that the recovery file is easy to assemble when we come to it. Claire, I'll leave you to decide how and when to take the recovery forward with the supplier.

If either of you needs anything further from me or from site to complete your files, let me know.

Tom
