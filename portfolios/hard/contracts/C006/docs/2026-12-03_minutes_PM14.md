## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

29,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Eastmarch Utilities representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

## 5. Design and technical

8 RFIs open, 3 closed in the period.

Pipe support schedule for the tank farm was checked against the isometric drawings. Some supports listed as guides are shown as anchors on the layout, which would change the stress model. The stress and support designers agreed to align the schedule and reissue it with a clear change list for the site team.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Commissioning certificates for the fire water pumps were checked against the test schedule. Flow and pressure results met the specified values and the controller tests were recorded. The original factory certificate was missing one page, so the vendor has been asked to send a complete copy for the handover file.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.

## 9. Any other business

Heavy rain over the weekend left standing water in the pipe rack corridor and on the access road beyond the second gate. Plant operators have been asked to check the surface before driving heavy equipment across. Temporary sump pumps will be relocated to the lowest point by the drainage contractor, and the site team will report any further flooding to the weekly safety forum.
