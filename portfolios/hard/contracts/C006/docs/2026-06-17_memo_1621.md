# Internal memo

**To:** James Mensah, Site Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Zoe Kerr, Planning Manager
**Date:** 17 June 2026
**Subject:** Format of operation and maintenance manuals for Area 100 Inlet

This memo sets out the format agreed for the operation and maintenance manuals for Area 100 Inlet, so that the sections can be drafted consistently by each discipline.

**File format**

- Each manual is to be issued as a single PDF with bookmarks linked to the main headings.
- Files must be named using the pattern Fennick Hydrogen Pilot-Area 100 Inlet-OM-XX, where XX is the volume number.
- Drawings are to be included in A3 format, folded to A4 and placed at the back of each volume.

**Structure**

1. Introduction and scope
2. Safety information and permits to work
3. Equipment descriptions, using the tag numbers from the master equipment list
4. Operating procedures, including start-up, shutdown and emergency isolation
5. Maintenance schedules and task sheets
6. Spare parts lists with supplier references
7. Certificates, test records and inspection reports

**Numbering and tagging**

- Equipment tag numbers must match the P&ID and the equipment register exactly. Do not abbreviate them.
- Page numbers should restart in each section.
- Revision history goes on the inside cover of each volume.

**Programme**

- Draft volumes 1 and 2 are due to Lucy Talbot by the end of next week.
- Volumes 3 to 5 follow two weeks after that.
- Each draft must be checked by the discipline lead before it is passed to document control.

Please send any questions to me directly. If a section cannot be completed because vendor data is still outstanding, flag it in the draft rather than leaving it blank.
