## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

32,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 400 Storage found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Aisha Moffat confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.

## 5. Design and technical

5 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

Equipment layout drawings for the new skid-mounted filtration units were marked up by the commissioning team. Their comments focus on isolation valve positions and drain points that are hard to reach. The layout designer will review the markups and reissue the drawing with revised access arrangements shown clearly for operators.

## 7. Quality

Fit-up inspection of the pipe spools for the sea water intake was completed before welding. Bevel angles, root gaps and alignment were measured and recorded. Two joints needed minor adjustment with a hydraulic clamp, and both were rechecked by the inspector before tacking was permitted.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Telecoms cabling for the temporary site office was damaged during trenching on Monday. The supplier has been called out and the repair is booked for tomorrow morning. Until then, the site office phone lines are down, so please use the mobile numbers on the noticeboard. Email access remains available through the portable satellite router.

## 9. Any other business

The welfare cabin near the pipe spool fabrication shop has run out of hot water twice this week. The boiler was found to be undersized for the number of people using it during the break periods. A second heater has been ordered and should be installed by the plumbing subcontractor before the end of next week.
