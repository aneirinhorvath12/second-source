## 2. Health, safety and environment

33,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.
