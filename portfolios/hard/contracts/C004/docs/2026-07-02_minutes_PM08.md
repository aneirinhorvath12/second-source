## 2. Health, safety and environment

26,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

## 7. Quality

The test certificate has been signed by both parties and the line is ready for reinstatement of the flanges.
