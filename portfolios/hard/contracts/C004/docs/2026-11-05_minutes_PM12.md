## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

18,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Owen Sinclair will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 6 closed in the period.

The control philosophy for the water treatment plant has been updated to reflect operator comments from the recent review. Changes include revised alarm priorities for the chemical dosing pumps and a new interlock on the filter backwash sequence. The updated document will go to Chloe Ellison for comment before it is adopted as the basis for the software build.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Pressure gauges used on the nitrogen purge skid were sent for recalibration to an accredited laboratory. Certificates have now been returned and checked against the instrument register. Three gauges drifted outside tolerance and have been withdrawn, with replacements fitted and recorded on the calibration log held by Isla Sutherland.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Gareth Brennan so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Heavy rain over the weekend left standing water in the pipe rack corridor and on the access road beyond the second gate. Plant operators have been asked to check the surface before driving heavy equipment across. Temporary sump pumps will be relocated to the lowest point by the drainage contractor, and the site team will report any further flooding to the weekly safety forum.
