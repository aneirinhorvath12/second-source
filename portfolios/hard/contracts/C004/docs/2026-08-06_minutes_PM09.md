## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Owen Sinclair will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 5. Design and technical

5 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 7. Quality

Material traceability for the carbon steel flanges was reviewed in the stores office. Heat numbers on several boxes were partly illegible due to weather exposure. The storekeeper transferred the information from the mill certificates, marked the boxes again and added a note to the stock record.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The customer has asked for the sewer connection inspection to be booked before the drainage trench is backfilled. Chloe Ellison has confirmed the inspector will attend on Friday at ten. Please leave the trench open and the pipework visible, and keep the access ladder in place so the inspector can view the joints safely.

## 9. Any other business

A reminder that all hot work permits must be countersigned by the area authority before the grinder or welding set is brought into Area 500 Export. Several permits last week were found to be missing the gas test record. Permit holders should bring their own gas detector to the morning briefing, and fire watch cover must continue for thirty minutes after the final task has finished.
