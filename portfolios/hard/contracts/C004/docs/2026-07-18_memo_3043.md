# Internal memo

**To:** Amira Nair, Contracts Manager, Ridgeway Civils
**From:** Gareth Brennan, Site Manager
**Date:** 18 July 2026
**Subject:** Reminder: personal protective equipment standards on Arden Valley, Warwickshire

This memo is a routine reminder of the minimum personal protective equipment (PPE) standards that apply across Arden Valley, Warwickshire. Please share it with all operatives on your next toolbox talk and confirm that it has been done.

**Minimum requirements in all work areas**

- Safety helmets to EN 397, with chin straps fitted when working at height or in wind
- Safety footwear to EN ISO 20345, rated S3 as a minimum
- High-visibility clothing to EN ISO 20471, Class 2 minimum, worn at all times outside site offices
- Eye protection to EN 166 for any cutting, grinding, chipping or pressurised testing

**Additional requirements by task**

- Hearing protection to EN 352 within the marked noise zones in Area 600 Control Building
- Gloves suited to the task, with chemical-resistant gloves for flushing and hydrotesting
- Fall arrest harnesses inspected before each use, with the inspection tag recorded

**Checks**

The site team will carry out spot checks each morning this week. Operatives found without the correct PPE will be stood down until it is corrected, and the incident will be logged on the daily report.

If any of your operatives have damaged or expired PPE, please replace it before they start work. Our stores can issue replacements on request.

Please direct any questions to me in the site office.
