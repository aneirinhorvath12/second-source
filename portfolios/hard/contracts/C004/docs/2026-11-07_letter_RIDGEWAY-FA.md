# Ridgeway Civils

**To:** Priya Talbot, Commercial Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**Date:** 07 November 2026

**Subject:** Civil works package: final account claim

Dear Priya,

I write on behalf of Ridgeway Civils regarding the civil works package at Arden Valley.

Ridgeway Civils submits a claim for disruption and prolongation on the civil works package in the sum of £913,000. This is exclusive of VAT. The detailed build-up, with supporting programme extracts and our site records, will follow under separate cover so that your quantity surveying team can begin checking it against your own records.

The claim arises from two matters, both of which are within Brackmoor's control rather than ours.

**Late access to work faces.** Our programme was prepared on the basis that work faces would be released to us in the order and at the times shown in the subcontract programme. In practice, a number of areas were handed over later than planned, in some cases substantially so. Our gangs were mobilised and ready, and on several occasions we held labour and plant on site without being able to start, or were able to start only part of the intended scope.

**Resequencing by Brackmoor.** Following the late releases, the order of working was changed by Brackmoor, and we were asked to move between areas at short notice. This meant demobilising from one location and remobilising at another, breaking up the continuity of work we had planned, and extending the time our supervision, temporary works and plant were required on site beyond what we had allowed for.

We have kept contemporaneous records throughout, including daily labour and plant returns and the access dates as we experienced them on the ground. Gareth Brennan's team will recognise most of the events described, and I would be happy for our site manager to sit down with him to go through the dates and reconcile them before the detailed submission is reviewed.

We would much prefer to resolve this commercially. I suggest that you, Ravi Davies and I meet in the next fortnight to go through the claim. If the matter is not resolved within 60 days of the date of this letter, Ridgeway Civils will refer it to adjudication. I mention this now so that there is no surprise, and not because we expect to need it.

Please acknowledge receipt, and let me have some dates when you and Ravi are available.

Yours sincerely,

Amira Nair
Contracts Manager
Ridgeway Civils
