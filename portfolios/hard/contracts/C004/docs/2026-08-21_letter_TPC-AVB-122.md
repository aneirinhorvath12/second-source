Further to the joint permit-to-work audit carried out on Area 200 Process on Tuesday, I set out below the findings for your records. - Isolation certificates were complete on nineteen of the twenty-two permits sampled. On three, the lock numbers did not match the isolation schedule and the verifying signature was missing.

- Attendance logs at the permit board were up to date at the day-shift handover but not at the night-shift changeover.
- Toolbox talks were held and signed for on every permit reviewed.
