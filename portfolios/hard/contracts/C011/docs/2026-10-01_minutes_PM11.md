## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

48,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Segregated walkways and vehicle routes have been reviewed around the new tanker bay. Delivery drivers were seen reversing without a banksman on two occasions last week. A second banksman will be rostered during peak delivery hours, and yellow lines have been repainted at the turning circle. Joanna Strachan has been notified of the change to the logistics route through the customer gate.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

Quality records for the pipe supports on Area 100 Inlet were audited against the drawing register. Several support drawings had been superseded without the earlier revision being withdrawn from site. The document controller has issued a list of affected drawings and the site team has been asked to remove obsolete copies from the work packs.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

A water main shutdown has been agreed with the local supplier for Tuesday between ten and two. Hydrants on the north side will be unavailable during this window, so the fire marshals need to be told. Please fill any hand-wash stations and check the site toilet cisterns before the shutdown begins, as the supply will be off.

## 9. Any other business

The welfare cabin near the pipe spool fabrication shop has run out of hot water twice this week. The boiler was found to be undersized for the number of people using it during the break periods. A second heater has been ordered and should be installed by the plumbing subcontractor before the end of next week.
