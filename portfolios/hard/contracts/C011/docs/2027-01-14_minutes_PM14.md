## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

39,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Portable appliance testing on the temporary power distribution boards is now 90 per cent complete. Three extension leads failed their insulation resistance test and were removed from site. Any lead that is damaged or has missing earthing must be taken out of use immediately and reported to the electrical supervisor. Test labels must be checked before tools are issued.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

## 5. Design and technical

12 RFIs open, 5 closed in the period.

Site engineers have checked the new layout against the setting-out survey and flagged two bolts that clash with an existing embedded plate. Aisha Mensah will attend the next walkdown to confirm the resolution.

## 7. Quality

Calibration of the flow meters on the custody transfer line was witnessed by Megan Nair. The meters were checked against a reference prover and the results were within the permitted error band. Certificates have been filed and the meter tag numbers matched to the instrument index.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Each contractor operative must now present a valid card at the turnstile and sign the controlled-area register. Escorted visitors will be issued with a red lanyard, and Aisha Mensah will check the register at the end of every working day.

The scaffolding subcontractor has been asked to re-inspect and tag the full run before the painters mobilise. Until then, the affected lifts are to be cordoned off and nobody is to work above the first lift without a written green tag.
