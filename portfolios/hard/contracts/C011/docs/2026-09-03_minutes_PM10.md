## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

42,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

## 7. Quality

Dimensional checks on the skid frame for the chemical dosing package were recorded against the approved fabrication drawings. Base plate levels were within tolerance, but the nozzle orientation on one vessel was rotated by about four degrees. The fabricator has been asked to submit a proposed correction for review by the design team.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The tanker carrying nitrogen for the purge system will park on the concrete pad beside the compressor house. The driver must wear the correct PPE and remain in the vehicle cab during connection. The pad has an oil interceptor, so please keep the drain covers in place and report any spillage to the duty supervisor immediately.

The fuel bowser is booked for Thursday evening, but the driver has asked for the access route to be cleared of plant. Ade Okafor is to arrange for the fencing contractor to move the temporary barrier so that the tanker can reach the refuelling point safely.
