## 2. Health, safety and environment

29,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Tregarth Groundworks will provide feedback at the next meeting.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.
