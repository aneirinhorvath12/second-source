# Internal memo

**To:** Owen Carver, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Ade Okafor, Site Manager
**Date:** 30 November 2026
**Subject:** Fire drill report: Area 500 Export and welfare block

This memo summarises the fire drill held on Thursday at 10:15 across Saltby, Teesside.

Attendance: 84 personnel on site at the time. Roll call was completed at the north field assembly point in 6 minutes 20 seconds against a target of 8 minutes. Aisha Mensah observed the roll call.

Observations:

- The alarm was clearly audible in the welfare block but not at the far end of Area 300 Utilities. An additional sounder is recommended on the pipe rack.
- Two operatives from Corvan Piping Services had not signed the site induction register, so their names were recorded manually at the assembly point.
- The north side fire marshal was not wearing a hi-vis tabard. This was replaced on the spot.
- The extinguisher at the main gate had an expired inspection tag. It has been replaced.
- Assembly point signage is faded and a replacement has been ordered.

Actions:

1. Electrician to check sounder coverage in Area 300 Utilities by next Friday.
2. Induction register to be checked at the gate every morning before the shift starts.
3. Fire marshal rota to be refreshed, with two named deputies per shift.

Joanna Strachan has been informed of the outcome and of the actions above.
