Dear Megan,

Please find enclosed Revision C of the traffic management plan for the main site entrance at Saltby, Teesside. The revision has been prepared following the trial of the temporary arrangement over the last month and comments from the local highway authority.

**Principal changes**

- The temporary give-way arrangement at the junction with the access road has been replaced by a signed stop line, with a banksman stationed at the gate during the morning and afternoon peaks.
- Delivery vehicles over 12 metres will now approach from the north only, using the revised route shown on drawing TM-03.
- A second holding area for up to four vehicles has been added inside the boundary fence, to stop vehicles queuing on the public highway.
- Pedestrian access for site staff has been moved to the western gate, with a segregated walkway marked along the boundary.
- The speed limit within the site has been reduced to 10 mph and is signed at both entrances.

**Swept path analysis**

The swept path analysis for the largest articulated vehicles is attached as Appendix B. It confirms the revised route can be used without the vehicles crossing the centre line of the access road.

**Next steps**

We would be grateful if you could review Revision C and confirm acceptance so the banksman rota and signage can be installed before the next major delivery. Our site manager, Ade Okafor, is available to walk the entrance with you at a time that suits.
