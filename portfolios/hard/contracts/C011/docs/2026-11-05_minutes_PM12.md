## 2. Health, safety and environment

34,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Liam Sinclair has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.
