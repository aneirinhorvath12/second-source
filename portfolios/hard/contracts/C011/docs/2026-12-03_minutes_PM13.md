## 2. Health, safety and environment

56,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Owen Carver will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

The LOLER thorough examination of the 25 tonne mobile crane is due next Thursday, and the current certificate was checked at the meeting. The hook block and wire rope inspection records are complete, but the outrigger pad condition report is still outstanding from the hire company. Until it is received, the crane will only be used within the limits of the existing load chart, and lifts will be planned accordingly.
