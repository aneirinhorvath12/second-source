Dear Owen,

I refer to your quotation BRK/SCC/Q/026 and have considered it with Joanna Strachan and with Nadia Pemberton.

The quotation describes the revised foundation arrangement for the absorber column, comprising the move from the spread footing shown in your Issue B design to a piled raft, together with the associated changes to the base slab reinforcement and the drainage falls around the column plinth.

Section 4.3 of the Employer's Requirements sets performance criteria for this foundation: a maximum total settlement, a limit on differential settlement across the column base, and the design wind and operating loads to be resisted. It does not prescribe a foundation type. The choice of a spread footing in Issue B was Brackmoor's, and it was made on the basis of the ground information available at that stage.

The ground investigation addendum, which your designers received in July, reported a softer layer of made ground beneath the footing position than the earlier boreholes had indicated. Your design checks then showed that the spread footing would not meet the differential settlement limit in the Employer's Requirements. The piled raft is the solution your designers have developed to meet that limit. The change to the reinforcement and to the falls around the plinth follows from the new foundation level and the pile cap geometry. None of it alters the criteria the foundation has to satisfy.

In our view this is the development of Brackmoor's own design to meet the Employer's Requirements. Under clause 2.3 the work forms part of the Contractor's obligations, and no adjustment to the Contract Price is due in respect of it. Please therefore treat the quotation as closed on that basis.

We have no comment on how Brackmoor sequences the piling with Tregarth Groundworks, but I would ask that the revised design is issued for review in the normal way, so that comments can be returned within the agreed review period.

Please contact me if you would like to talk through the reasoning above.
