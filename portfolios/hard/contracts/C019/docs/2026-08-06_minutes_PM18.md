## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

37,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Corvan Piping Services will provide feedback at the next meeting.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Eastmarch Utilities representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

Model review of the compressor building was carried out with Owen Carver and Megan Rowe present. Walkways and access routes were tested against the maintenance envelope requirements for the reciprocating machine. Two platforms were found to restrict lifting access, and the design team will propose revised steelwork to restore clear routes.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

A toolbox talk was held on the importance of hold point signatures and the consequences of closing work before inspection. Foremen were reminded that a hold point is a stop, not a formality. Attendance was recorded and the sheet was filed with the weekly safety and quality pack.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

A new laydown area has been set up behind the workshop for the bulk structural steel. The hardstanding is level, drained and fenced, but the gate lock has not yet been fitted. Until it is, please ensure the chain and padlock are secured each evening and that the key is returned to the site stores before the end of the shift.

## 9. Any other business

Vehicle movement near the crane lay down has been noted as too close to the exclusion zone on several occasions. Banksmen are reminded to hold their position at the edge of the marked boundary and not to step inside it while a lift is in progress. Dedicated signage will be placed at each corner by the end of the week.
