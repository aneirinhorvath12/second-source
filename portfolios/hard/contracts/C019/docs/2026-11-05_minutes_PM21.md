## 2. Health, safety and environment

32,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Portable appliance testing on the temporary power distribution boards is now 90 per cent complete. Three extension leads failed their insulation resistance test and were removed from site. Any lead that is damaged or has missing earthing must be taken out of use immediately and reported to the electrical supervisor. Test labels must be checked before tools are issued.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.

## 5. Design and technical

8 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Ravi Walsh before the joint was closed up.
