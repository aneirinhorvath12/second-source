# Strathcorr Compressor Station: progress meeting no. 23

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Owen Carver (ER), Craig Quigley, Megan Rowe, Priya Ellison (Eastmarch Utilities); Marcus Kowalski, Callum Ashworth, Dev Whitlock, Aisha Ferris (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

19,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

A spill response drill in the tank farm simulated a diesel release from a damaged transfer hose. The team deployed booms within eleven minutes, and the drain valves were closed as planned. Some confusion arose over who should contact the environment officer, so the call-out list at the bund gate will be updated. A debrief with all shift leaders is scheduled for Friday morning.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 69.5%. Activities progressed this period: Area 500 Export - Electrical - Install cable tray; Area 500 Export - Piping - Hydrotest (part 1 of 3); Area 300 Utilities - Electrical - Pull cables (part 3 of 3); Area 100 Inlet - Electrical - Terminate and test (part 2 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 14 December 2027 against contract completion 04 April 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

10 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

A request for information was raised by Orrin Commissioning this morning concerning the support spacing on the 150 mm carbon steel line running along the pipe rack in Area 400 Storage. The isometric shows supports at 3.0 m centres, whereas the stress report assumes 2.5 m. Ruth Mensah has asked the design team to confirm which basis governs and to issue a marked-up isometric before the next spool release meeting.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 23 submitted on 06 January 2027.

## 7. Quality

The welding procedure specification for the duplex stainless steel pipework was reissued following a change of filler metal supplier. Welders were briefed on the revised interpass temperature limits and the new procedure was posted at each welding station. Old copies were collected and destroyed to avoid confusion.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The insulated pipe spools for Area 600 Control Building are arriving in six separate loads over the next three days. Each load needs a pre-booked slot at the goods reception cabin. Drivers must bring the delivery note, the material certificate copy and the photo ID that matches the booking. Late arrivals will be held at the weighbridge until a slot is free.

## 9. Any other business

A reminder that all hot work permits must be countersigned by the area authority before the grinder or welding set is brought into Area 500 Export. Several permits last week were found to be missing the gas test record. Permit holders should bring their own gas detector to the morning briefing, and fire watch cover must continue for thirty minutes after the final task has finished.
