**Subject:** Archaeological watching brief update, Strathcorr, Aberdeenshire access road

Dear Owen,

I am writing to update you on the archaeological watching brief for the new access road at Strathcorr, Aberdeenshire. The archaeologist appointed under the written scheme of investigation has been on site for the groundworks in the north-west corner since the start of the month.

## Progress

Topsoil stripping has been completed across the section between chainage 0 and chainage 180. The archaeologist observed the works and recorded the exposed surface. No significant archaeological deposits were found in this section. A small quantity of modern brick and clay pipe fragments was recovered from the topsoil and has been bagged for the record.

## Findings

At chainage 220, a linear feature was exposed beneath the subsoil. The archaeologist has recorded it in plan and section and has advised that it may be a field boundary. Further excavation has been paused at this point to allow the feature to be recorded fully. The works have been re-sequenced so that the adjacent drainage trench can proceed while the recording is completed.

## Next steps

- The archaeologist will complete the record for the linear feature by the end of next week.
- Brackmoor will keep the watching brief in place for all ground disturbance within the defined area.
- A written summary will be issued to the county archaeology team once the fieldwork is complete.

I will send you the recording sheets and photographs when they are available. Please let me know if you would like to visit the trench before it is backfilled.
