Further to the walkdown on Tuesday, I am writing to confirm the requirements for the perimeter hoardings along the Strathcorr, Aberdeenshire frontage and the eastern boundary by Area 600 Control Building.

The customer's brand guidelines apply to all visible hoarding faces. In summary:

- Panels to be pre-finished in the approved corporate blue, matching the reference chip held by Megan Rowe.
- Logo panels to be positioned 1.8 m above footpath level, centred on each bay, with no logo cut across a panel joint.
- Graphics to be printed on weather-resistant vinyl and fixed with tamper-resistant fixings only.
- Any damaged or faded panels to be replaced within two working days of being reported by Megan Rowe.

Please confirm that your hoarding supplier can meet the finish and logo positioning before the next panel delivery is booked. I would also ask that a sample panel is installed at the site entrance so that the colour and print can be checked in daylight before the remaining panels are fixed.

The public safety signage shown on the drawings remains a Brackmoor responsibility and must not be altered by the branding scheme. Where the two conflict, please raise it with me before fixing rather than after.
