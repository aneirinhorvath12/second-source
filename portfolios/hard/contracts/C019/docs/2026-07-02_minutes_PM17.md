## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

24,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Dev Whitlock will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Ruth Mensah before the joint was closed up.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The tanker carrying nitrogen for the purge system will park on the concrete pad beside the compressor house. The driver must wear the correct PPE and remain in the vehicle cab during connection. The pad has an oil interceptor, so please keep the drain covers in place and report any spillage to the duty supervisor immediately.

## 9. Any other business

Owen Carver has asked for the updated list of authorised switching personnel by Wednesday. Until the list is circulated, no one should accept a permit for switching on the 11kV panels.
