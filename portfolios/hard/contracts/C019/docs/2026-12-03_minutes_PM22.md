## 2. Health, safety and environment

38,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Surface water discharge from the attenuation pond was sampled on Monday and sent for laboratory analysis. Results are awaited, and the pond outlet valve remains closed until they are received. Silt fences along the access road have been inspected and two sections need repair after heavy rain. Dev Whitlock has asked the drainage team to clear the gully gratings before the next forecast rain.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.
