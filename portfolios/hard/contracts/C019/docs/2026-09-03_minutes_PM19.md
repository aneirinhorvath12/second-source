## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

46,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 5. Design and technical

7 RFIs open, 5 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 600 Control Building identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Aisha Ferris first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

Tregarth Groundworks managers are to ensure their supervisors complete the sheet before leaving the gatehouse, and the site office will spot check the records each week.
