## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Portable appliance testing on the temporary power distribution boards is now 90 per cent complete. Three extension leads failed their insulation resistance test and were removed from site. Any lead that is damaged or has missing earthing must be taken out of use immediately and reported to the electrical supervisor. Test labels must be checked before tools are issued.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.

## 7. Quality

Visual inspection of the stainless steel vessel internals was carried out before the manway covers were refitted. The inspector found a small amount of weld spatter on a support ring and recorded it on the inspection report. Cleaning and passivation of the affected area will be witnessed before the vessel is closed.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The customer's security team has reviewed the new access card process for the control room extension. Each contractor operative must now present a valid card at the turnstile and sign the controlled-area register. Escorted visitors will be issued with a red lanyard, and Megan Rowe will check the register at the end of every working day.

## 9. Any other business

Vehicle movement near the crane lay down has been noted as too close to the exclusion zone on several occasions. Banksmen are reminded to hold their position at the edge of the marked boundary and not to step inside it while a lift is in progress. Dedicated signage will be placed at each corner by the end of the week.
