# Brackmoor Engineering Ltd

**To:** Owen Carver, Employer's Representative, Eastmarch Utilities
**Date:** 01 January 2027
**Our ref:** BRK/SCS/L/0434
**Project:** Strathcorr Compressor Station
**Subject:** First aid cover arrangements over the Christmas and New Year shutdown

Dear Owen,

I am writing to confirm the first aid arrangements that will apply at Strathcorr, Aberdeenshire during the planned shutdown period, when the main workforce will be reduced and only essential activities will continue.

From the 22nd of December until the 2nd of January, we will maintain a minimum of two first aiders on site at all times while any work is under way. Both will hold current three-day emergency first aid at work certificates, and their names and contact numbers will be posted at the main gate, in the site office and in the welfare block.

The first aid room will remain stocked and the defibrillator will be checked on the first working day of the shutdown. The eyewash stations on Area 600 Control Building will be inspected before the reduced crew mobilises, and any shortfalls will be replaced immediately.

As in previous holidays, we will not permit hot work or confined space entry on the days when only one first aider is rostered, and Dev Whitlock will be the single point of contact for any incident during the break. He can be reached on the site emergency line, which will be forwarded to his mobile phone out of hours.

Please let me know if Eastmarch Utilities requires any additional cover, or if you would like a copy of the rota before the shutdown begins.

Yours sincerely,

Marcus Kowalski
Project Manager
Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
