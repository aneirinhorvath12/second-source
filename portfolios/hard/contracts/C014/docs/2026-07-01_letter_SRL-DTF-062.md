I write as Employer's Representative to issue instruction ERI-032 under clause 14 of the Contract.

**Scope of the instruction**

Brackmoor is instructed to carry out additional earthing and lightning protection to the new standard, as follows:

- Extend the buried earth grid to cover the full bunded area of the tank farm, with each tank shell bonded to the grid at not fewer than two diametrically opposite points.
- Provide lightning protection to the new standard for the tanks, the pump house and the gantry walkway, together with test points at each down conductor to allow isolated resistance testing.
- Bond the pipework, cable tray and all metallic structures within the bund to the extended grid, including at flanged joints where continuity is not otherwise assured.

**Quotation**

Please submit a quotation under clause 14.2 within 14 days of the date of this letter. The quotation should set out the proposed adjustment to the Contract Sum, any effect on the programme and the Completion Date, and the basis on which each has been assessed. Please also identify any part of the instructed work that Brackmoor considers it has already priced, or that falls within its existing design obligations, so that this can be reviewed alongside the quotation.
