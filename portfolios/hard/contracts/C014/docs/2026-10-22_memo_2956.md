# Internal memo

**To:** Laura Quigley, Project Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Ruth Pemberton, Site Manager
**Date:** 22 October 2026
**Subject:** Fire drill report: Area 400 Storage and welfare block

This memo summarises the fire drill held on Thursday at 10:15 across Dunmere, Cornwall.

Attendance: 84 personnel on site at the time. Roll call was completed at the north field assembly point in 6 minutes 20 seconds against a target of 8 minutes. Euan Whitlock observed the roll call.

Observations:

- The alarm was clearly audible in the welfare block but not at the far end of Area 200 Process. An additional sounder is recommended on the pipe rack.
- Two operatives from Ridgeway Civils had not signed the site induction register, so their names were recorded manually at the assembly point.
- The north side fire marshal was not wearing a hi-vis tabard. This was replaced on the spot.
- The extinguisher at the main gate had an expired inspection tag. It has been replaced.
- Assembly point signage is faded and a replacement has been ordered.

Actions:

1. Electrician to check sounder coverage in Area 200 Process by next Friday.
2. Induction register to be checked at the gate every morning before the shift starts.
3. Fire marshal rota to be refreshed, with two named deputies per shift.

Gareth Morgan has been informed of the outcome and of the actions above.
