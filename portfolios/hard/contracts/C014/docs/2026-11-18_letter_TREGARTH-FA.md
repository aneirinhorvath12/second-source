# Tregarth Groundworks

**To:** Fatima Walsh, Commercial Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**Date:** 18 November 2026

**Subject:** Groundworks and drainage package: final account claim

Dear Fatima,

We write in connection with the groundworks and drainage package at Dunmere Tank Farm and submit, under cover of this letter, Tregarth Groundworks' claim for disruption and prolongation on that package, in the sum of £710,000.

The claim arises from two causes. The first is late access to work faces. Throughout the package we were mobilised and ready to start on the areas shown on the programme, but on a number of occasions the faces were not available on the dates we had planned our gangs and plant around. Our site diaries record the dates on which each area was actually released to us, and the supporting schedule sets these against the dates in the programme issued to us at the start of the works.

The second cause is resequencing by Brackmoor. Our work was moved out of the order in which we had priced and planned it, and in several instances we were asked to demobilise from one area and return to it later. This meant repeated moves of plant, labour and temporary works, and prevented us from working the drainage runs in the continuous way for which the package was set up. The supporting schedule sets out the instances by area, with the daily records and the relevant programme revisions behind each.

The claim document comprises a narrative, the schedule of access dates, the resequencing record, and our build-up of the sum claimed. Our site team is happy to take your Senior Quantity Surveyor through the records, and I would suggest Hannah Mensah and I arrange a session at Dunmere, ideally with Ruth Pemberton present, since she will remember most of the movements first-hand.

We would much prefer to settle this by agreement and are ready to meet at your earliest convenience. However, I must make clear that if the matter is not resolved within 60 days of the date of this letter, Tregarth Groundworks will refer it to adjudication.

Please acknowledge receipt, and let me have a date for the meeting. If any of the records referred to above are not in the bundle you receive, tell me and I will have them sent over the same day.

Yours sincerely,

Bethan Yates
Contracts Manager
Tregarth Groundworks
