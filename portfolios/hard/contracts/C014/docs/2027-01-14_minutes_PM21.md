## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

40,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Quigley will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 67.0%. Activities progressed this period: Area 300 Utilities - Mechanical - Install package skids; Area 300 Utilities - Instrumentation - Install instruments; Area 500 Export - Piping - Fabricate spools (part 2 of 2); Area 100 Inlet - Electrical - Pull cables (part 3 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 31 January 2028 against contract completion 18 May 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 4 closed in the period.

Equipment layout drawings for the new skid-mounted filtration units were marked up by the commissioning team. Their comments focus on isolation valve positions and drain points that are hard to reach. The layout designer will review the markups and reissue the drawing with revised access arrangements shown clearly for operators.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 21 submitted on 08 January 2027.

## 7. Quality

Fit-up inspection of the pipe spools for the sea water intake was completed before welding. Bevel angles, root gaps and alignment were measured and recorded. Two joints needed minor adjustment with a hydraulic clamp, and both were rechecked by the inspector before tacking was permitted.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Nadia Strachan first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

The jetty tide window for the cable drum offload has moved to the second week of next month because of the marine coordinator's schedule. Gareth Morgan has been informed and has agreed in principle. Lifting crews are asked to keep their availability open for that period, and the marine contractor will confirm the exact dates by Friday.
