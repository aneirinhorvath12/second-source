**To:** Gareth Morgan, Project Manager, Sorrel Renewables
**Date:** 06 October 2026
**Our ref:** BRK/DTF/L/0348
**Project:** Dunmere Tank Farm
**Subject:** Lessons learned: minor plant collision at Area 500 Export

Dear Gareth,

I am writing to share the outcome of the review into the minor plant collision at Area 200 Process last week, and the actions we have taken.

A telehandler reversing near the skid frame struck the handrail on the access walkway. No one was injured, and the only damage was a scuffed and bent section of handrail, which has been replaced. The operator was working alone and did not have a banksman in position, and the reverse alarm was not audible over the compressor noise nearby.

The review found three contributing factors:

- the lift route had not been planned against the plant layout drawing;
- the exclusion zone around the skid had been removed to allow a pipe delivery and was not reinstated;
- the operator had not been briefed on the revised walkway layout.

We have taken the following actions:

- a banksman is now required for all reversing movements within 15 metres of process equipment, and this is written into the lifting plan template;
- the exclusion zone is to be reinstated at the end of each delivery, checked by the shift supervisor before work continues;
- all plant operators have been briefed on the revised walkway layout, with the drawing signed as read;
- a rear-view camera is being fitted to the telehandler on hire.

We would be grateful if you could share the summary with Euan Whitlock and the other contractors on the site, so the banksman requirement applies to everyone working around the skid area. I will bring the closure record to the next site meeting.
