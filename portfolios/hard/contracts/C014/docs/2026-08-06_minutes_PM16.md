## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

43,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Quigley will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

The daily quality walkdown on the jetty headstock identified missing identification tags on four valve actuators. Tags were replaced from the vendor kit and the tag register updated. ## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Fire hydrant access on the east side of the unit must stay clear at all times. Two pallets of valves were found blocking the hydrant on Tuesday, and the fire officer has asked for the area to be signed. Please keep the route to the hydrant open so that a fire tender can reach it within two minutes of an alarm.

## 9. Any other business

The noise survey for the water treatment plant will be repeated next week because some readings were taken during a period of heavy plant use. Crews are asked to keep the rotary drills and the diesel compressor off the plant between 10:00 and 11:30 on the survey days. The acoustic consultant will issue the timetable on Monday.
