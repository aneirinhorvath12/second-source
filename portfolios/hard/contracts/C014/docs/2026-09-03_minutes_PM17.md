## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

59,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Laura Quigley has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Quigley will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 53.1%. Activities progressed this period: Area 400 Storage - Civils - Pour foundations (part 2 of 3); Area 100 Inlet - Mechanical - Install package skids; Area 400 Storage - Piping - Fabricate spools (part 2 of 2); Area 400 Storage - Structural - Erect primary steel.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 31 January 2028 against contract completion 18 May 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 3 closed in the period.

Piping stress analysis for the hot oil line feeding the tank farm has been completed. Nozzle loads on the pump discharge are slightly above the vendor limits in the thermal case. Options include adding a spring hanger or relocating the adjacent anchor. The stress engineer will present both options at the design meeting on Thursday.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Weekly quality meeting minutes were circulated to the site team and the customer representatives. Key points included a rise in minor NCRs relating to paint touch-ups, a request for better labelling of spool pieces, and confirmation that the calibration register will be audited next month by Kieran Ferris.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

A new laydown area has been set up behind the workshop for the bulk structural steel. The hardstanding is level, drained and fenced, but the gate lock has not yet been fitted. Until it is, please ensure the chain and padlock are secured each evening and that the key is returned to the site stores before the end of the shift.

## 9. Any other business

Vehicle movement near the crane lay down has been noted as too close to the exclusion zone on several occasions. Banksmen are reminded to hold their position at the edge of the marked boundary and not to step inside it while a lift is in progress. Dedicated signage will be placed at each corner by the end of the week.
