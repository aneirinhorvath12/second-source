## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

31,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Quigley will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 10 closed in the period.

A technical query from Kestrel Cranes concerns the material specification for the gaskets on the sulphuric acid line. The drawing calls for a PTFE-based gasket, but the purchase specification lists a graphite option. Engineering has been asked to confirm the chemical compatibility and to issue a single approved specification to both teams.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 400 Storage identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The tanker carrying nitrogen for the purge system will park on the concrete pad beside the compressor house. The driver must wear the correct PPE and remain in the vehicle cab during connection. The pad has an oil interceptor, so please keep the drain covers in place and report any spillage to the duty supervisor immediately.

## 9. Any other business

A reminder that all hot work permits must be countersigned by the area authority before the grinder or welding set is brought into Area 500 Export. Several permits last week were found to be missing the gas test record. Permit holders should bring their own gas detector to the morning briefing, and fire watch cover must continue for thirty minutes after the final task has finished.
