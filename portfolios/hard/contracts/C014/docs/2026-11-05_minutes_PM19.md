## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

57,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Sorrel Renewables representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

Harness inspection records for Ridgeway Civils show that eleven fall arrest harnesses were removed from service during the month because of frayed stitching or damaged buckles. Replacements have been issued and the old harnesses have been cut up and disposed of. Each worker must present their harness for a pre-use check before going onto the pipe rack, and supervisors will log the checks on the daily permit.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Commissioning certificates for the fire water pumps were checked against the test schedule. Flow and pressure results met the specified values and the controller tests were recorded. The original factory certificate was missing one page, so the vendor has been asked to send a complete copy for the handover file.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The temporary power supply to the welfare compound will be switched over on Monday afternoon. The distribution board was tested by the electrician last Friday and the earth continuity readings were recorded. Anyone working on the site office kettles, heaters or chargers should unplug them before the changeover at fourteen hundred hours.

## 9. Any other business

The welfare cabin near the pipe spool fabrication shop has run out of hot water twice this week. The boiler was found to be undersized for the number of people using it during the break periods. A second heater has been ordered and should be installed by the plumbing subcontractor before the end of next week.
