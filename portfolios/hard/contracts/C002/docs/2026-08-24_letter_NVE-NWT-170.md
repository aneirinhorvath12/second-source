**Description of the change**

The instruction is for upgraded fire and gas detection to the Employer's revised standard. In outline:

- The detection philosophy changes from the current zoned arrangement to addressable devices on a loop, with each device individually identified at the control panel.
- Point infrared hydrocarbon gas detectors replace the catalytic bead units shown in the current design for the chemical dosing area and the blower house. Flame detection is to be multi-spectrum infrared.
- The fire and gas panel is to provide a hard-wired shutdown signal and a serial link to the site control system, with detector coverage mapping updated to suit the revised layout.

The Employer's revised standard is enclosed with this letter. Please check it against the design information you currently hold and let me know promptly if anything appears to be missing.
