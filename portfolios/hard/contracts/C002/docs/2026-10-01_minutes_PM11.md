**Date:** 01 October 2026, 10:00, site offices

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

47,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Laura Hale will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Daniel Bryant has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 28.1%. Activities progressed this period: Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Pour foundations; Area 200 Process - Civils - Backfill and compact (part 2 of 3); Area 400 Storage - Civils - Pour foundations; Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 2 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 02 October 2028 against contract completion 16 April 2029.

## 5. Design and technical

10 RFIs open, 5 closed in the period.

Revised general arrangement drawings for the water treatment plant were reviewed with the operations team. They asked for clearer labelling of isolation valves, larger access clearances around the lime silo, and a revised location for the eyewash station. The drawing office will incorporate these comments into the next issue for approval.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 11 submitted on 28 September 2026.

## 7. Quality

Commissioning certificates for the fire water pumps were checked against the test schedule. Flow and pressure results met the specified values and the controller tests were recorded. The original factory certificate was missing one page, so the vendor has been asked to send a complete copy for the handover file.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Lucy Ellison first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

Heavy rain over the weekend left standing water in the pipe rack corridor and on the access road beyond the second gate. Plant operators have been asked to check the surface before driving heavy equipment across. Temporary sump pumps will be relocated to the lowest point by the drainage contractor, and the site team will report any further flooding to the weekly safety forum.
