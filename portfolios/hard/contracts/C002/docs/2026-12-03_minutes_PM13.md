## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Laura Hale will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 5. Design and technical

10 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

A model clash review covering the chemical dosing area highlighted interference between a cable tray and a new sample line. The team agreed that the sample line should move, as access to the tray is needed more often. The piping designer will amend the routing and the isometric will be reissued after the next model update.

## 7. Quality

Pressure gauges used on the nitrogen purge skid were sent for recalibration to an accredited laboratory. Certificates have now been returned and checked against the instrument register. Three gauges drifted outside tolerance and have been withdrawn, with replacements fitted and recorded on the calibration log held by Holly Sutherland.

Please keep the lifting zone clear of all personnel and vehicles from six in the morning until the bundle is landed and secured.
