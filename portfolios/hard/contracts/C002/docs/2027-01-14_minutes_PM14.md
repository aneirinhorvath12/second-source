## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

33,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 200 Process found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Fiona Kerr confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

The HAZOP action log has been reviewed against the current P&IDs. Design leads have been asked to incorporate these items into the next issue and to record closure in the action tracker.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Test pack 14 for the gas compression module covers instrument air, nitrogen and fuel gas tie-ins. Loop checks on twenty-two transmitters are recorded as complete, with calibration certificates attached. Outstanding items include the wiring labels on the marshalling cabinet and a final continuity check on the emergency shutdown circuit.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Our neighbour on the eastern boundary has asked for deliveries to avoid the school run between eight and nine in the morning. Graham Okafor has agreed that all heavy vehicles will stage at the lay-by until ten past nine. Drivers will be told at the induction, and the security team will radio the gate when each vehicle is released.

## 9. Any other business

Scaffold inspection tags on the tank farm bund were found to be out of date on the east elevation. The scaffolding subcontractor has been asked to re-inspect and tag the full run before the painters mobilise. Until then, the affected lifts are to be cordoned off and nobody is to work above the first lift without a written green tag.
