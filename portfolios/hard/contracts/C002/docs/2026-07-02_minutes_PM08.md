## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

32,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Priya Talbot will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 20.4%. Activities progressed this period: Area 400 Storage - Civils - Excavate and blind; Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 1 of 3); Area 200 Process - Civils - Pour foundations (part 1 of 3); Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Backfill and compact.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 02 October 2028 against contract completion 16 April 2029.

## 5. Design and technical

8 RFIs open, 8 closed in the period.

Pressure testing boundaries on the hydrogen service piping were reviewed against the latest P&IDs. Several spool pieces appear to be missing from the test packs, and two blind flanges are shown in the wrong position. The test engineer has asked for corrected test boundary drawings before the next pneumatic test package is prepared.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 8 submitted on 25 June 2026.

## 7. Quality

Calibration of the flow meters on the custody transfer line was witnessed by Bethan Achebe. The meters were checked against a reference prover and the results were within the permitted error band. Certificates have been filed and the meter tag numbers matched to the instrument index.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The overhead line crossing the site entrance has been identified as a risk to tall vehicles. Height barriers will be installed at the approach and the warning signs repositioned. Drivers of tipper trucks and mobile cranes must check the clearance before entering, and any vehicle that cannot pass safely must be turned around at the lay-by.

## 9. Any other business

The gas detection system in Area 500 Export has been generating nuisance alarms during the morning cleaning rounds. Engineers suspect a fault in one of the portable reference units rather than a real leak. The unit has been removed for bench checking and a spare has been issued. Operators must still respond to every alarm as normal until the cause is confirmed.
