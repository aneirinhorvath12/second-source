**Date:** 06 August 2026, 10:00, site offices

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

49,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 23.4%. Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Pour foundations (part 1 of 2); Area 200 Process - Civils - Excavate and blind (part 1 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Civils - Excavate and blind (part 1 of 3); Area 200 Process - Structural - Erect primary steel.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 02 October 2028 against contract completion 16 April 2029.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

The commissioning team raised a query about the vent line sizing on the methanol storage tank. The current line appears undersized for the maximum breathing load in the event of rapid filling. The process engineer is checking the calculation and will confirm whether the line size or the filling rate should change.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 9 submitted on 31 July 2026.

## 7. Quality

Weekly quality meeting minutes were circulated to the site team and the customer representatives. Key points included a rise in minor NCRs relating to paint touch-ups, a request for better labelling of spool pieces, and confirmation that the calibration register will be audited next month by Bethan Achebe.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Laydown area C is being cleared to receive the pump skids and the associated electrical kiosks. The ground has been compacted and sleepers placed under the outriggers of the mobile crane. Please do not stack any timber or drums in this zone without checking with Lucy Ellison first, as the bearing capacity has only been confirmed for the listed loads.

## 9. Any other business

Waste segregation at the laydown yard is still not meeting the agreed standard. Timber offcuts, metal cuttings and oily rags are being placed in the same skip. A separate skip for each waste stream has been ordered and will arrive on Monday. Until then, foremen are asked to walk their gangs through the labelled bins at the start of each shift.
