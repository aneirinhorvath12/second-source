## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

19,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.

## 5. Design and technical

12 RFIs open, 6 closed in the period.

The electrical earthing layout for the new substation was checked against the design basis. The earth grid spacing matches the calculation, but the connection points to the transformer neutral are not clearly labelled. A revised drawing has been requested, and the earthing designer will confirm that all bonding conductors are shown with correct sizes.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Our neighbour on the eastern boundary has asked for deliveries to avoid the school run between eight and nine in the morning. Holly Sutherland has agreed that all heavy vehicles will stage at the lay-by until ten past nine. Drivers will be told at the induction, and the security team will radio the gate when each vehicle is released.

## 9. Any other business

The welfare cabin near the pipe spool fabrication shop has run out of hot water twice this week. The boiler was found to be undersized for the number of people using it during the break periods. A second heater has been ordered and should be installed by the plumbing subcontractor before the end of next week.
