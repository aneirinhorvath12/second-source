**Subject:** Corran Point Desalination - Attempted theft of copper earthing cable, night of 3rd to 4th

Dear Hannah,

I am writing to report an attempted theft of copper earthing cable from the cable tray route on the east side of Corran Point, Argyll, discovered by the night security patrol at approximately 02:40 on the morning of 4th.

From the initial inspection, the perimeter fence had been cut at the access gate to the laydown area, and a section of approximately 15 metres of tray-mounted earthing conductor had been partly stripped and cut. The cable had not been removed from the site. The local police attended, took a statement and have given us a crime reference number, which I have attached.

**Immediate actions taken**

- The affected section has been isolated and made safe by our electrical team, with a permit to work issued.
- The gate and fence have been repaired temporarily and a temporary security barrier installed.
- Additional mobile patrols have been put in place for the next two weeks, covering the laydown area and the cable routes.
- All cable reels and loose copper stock in the laydown area have been moved into the secured compound.

**Actions to follow**

We will carry out a full inspection of the earthing system before re-energising the affected section, with the results recorded on the commissioning certificate. A review of the access control arrangements at the gate will be held with Holly Sutherland next Tuesday.

I will confirm the outcome of the inspection in writing once it is complete.

Yours sincerely,
