## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

38,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 600 Control Building found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Callum Haddad confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.

## 5. Design and technical

14 RFIs open, 5 closed in the period.

A request for information was raised by Tregarth Groundworks this morning concerning the support spacing on the 150 mm carbon steel line running along the pipe rack in Area 400 Storage. The isometric shows supports at 3.0 m centres, whereas the stress report assumes 2.5 m. Sophie Ashworth has asked the design team to confirm which basis governs and to issue a marked-up isometric before the next spool release meeting.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Positive material identification was carried out on twelve spool pieces delivered to laydown at Corran Point, Argyll. Eleven matched the material certificates supplied by the vendor. One spool showed a chromium reading lower than expected and has been quarantined in the stores compound pending a retest by an independent laboratory.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Gate 2 will be closed to light vehicles on Saturday morning while the pipe rack steelwork is craned over the boundary fence. Site security has been asked to redirect deliveries through Gate 1 and to log all arrivals in the visitor book. Signage is being placed at the junction with Mill Lane for the duration of the lift.

## 9. Any other business

Site induction numbers have grown sharply with the arrival of the commissioning team. Some visitors have been signing in without completing the online module. Reception marshals have been asked to check each name against the induction log before issuing a pass. Anyone unable to show a valid card should be escorted to the training cabin.
