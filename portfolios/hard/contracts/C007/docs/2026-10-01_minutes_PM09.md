## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

38,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Grace Bryant will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

14 RFIs open, 4 closed in the period.

Several gusset plates appear to conflict with the revised beam sizes shown in the latest general arrangement. The structural engineer has agreed to reissue the connection schedule, and erectors will hold work on those bays until the new details are confirmed.

## 7. Quality

Positive material identification was carried out on twelve spool pieces delivered to laydown at Corran Point, Argyll. Eleven matched the material certificates supplied by the vendor. One spool showed a chromium reading lower than expected and has been quarantined in the stores compound pending a retest by an independent laboratory.

The temporary traffic lights must be set to run on the approved sequence, and the signage must be checked each morning. Grace Bryant has asked the banksman team to log any near misses and report back before the end of each shift.

Operatives are reminded that the walkway is closed and that the alternative route via the service stair must be used. Any person found on the walkway during the closure will be removed from the site for the rest of the day.
