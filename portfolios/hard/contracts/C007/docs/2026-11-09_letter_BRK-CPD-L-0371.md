**Subject:** Site cleanliness and housekeeping standards, Corran Point, Argyll

Dear Claire,

Following your site inspection on Monday, I am writing to set out the housekeeping actions we will take across Corran Point, Argyll over the next fortnight.

Your inspection noted that waste skips in the laydown area were overfilled, that some cable drums were left in the walkways, and that the welding area near the main rack had accumulated offcuts and packaging. We accept these observations and have already acted on them.

The following measures are now in place:

- Skips will be emptied at the end of each shift, and the site supervisor will check them before the end of each working day.
- Cable drums and materials will be stored only in marked bays, and walkways will be kept clear at all times.
- Each subcontractor has been asked to nominate a housekeeper for their work area, who will carry out a daily sweep and report any problems to the site office.
- A weekly housekeeping walk-round will take place every Friday with Ian Talbot, and the results will be shared with you.

We will also review the waste segregation signage at the gate and make sure that oily rags are placed in the designated metal bins.

Please let me know if you would like to join the Friday walk-round, or if there are particular areas you would like us to concentrate on.

Yours sincerely,

Grace Bryant
Project Manager
Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
