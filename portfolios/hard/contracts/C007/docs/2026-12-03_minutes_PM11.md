## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

The fire drill on Wednesday evacuated the whole construction compound in just under six minutes, which is within target. However, two welfare cabins had their fire doors propped open with bricks, and the assembly point roll call took longer than expected because the sign-in sheets were incomplete. Access control and sign-in will be reinforced before the next drill.

The weekly housekeeping inspection of Area 300 Utilities found cable drums stored on the access route to the compressor skid, partly blocking the emergency egress. Two spill kits were found without lids and one fire extinguisher had an expired inspection tag. The site team cleared the route the same afternoon, and Ravi Davies confirmed that his operatives will check their laydown areas at the start of each shift for the rest of the month.

## 7. Quality

Material traceability for the carbon steel flanges was reviewed in the stores office. Heat numbers on several boxes were partly illegible due to weather exposure. The storekeeper transferred the information from the mill certificates, marked the boxes again and added a note to the stock record.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The customer's security team has reviewed the new access card process for the control room extension. Each contractor operative must now present a valid card at the turnstile and sign the controlled-area register. Escorted visitors will be issued with a red lanyard, and Claire Iqbal will check the register at the end of every working day.
