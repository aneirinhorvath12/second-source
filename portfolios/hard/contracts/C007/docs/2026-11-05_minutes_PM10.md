## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

28,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 5 closed in the period.

Gas detector placement on the compressor deck was reviewed against the detection philosophy. Several detectors sit close to a vent stack, which could affect reliability in cross-wind conditions. The process safety engineer will confirm whether relocation is needed, and the layout will be updated once the detector coverage calculation has been rerun.

## 7. Quality

The coating holiday test on the buried pipeline section was carried out at the trench bottom before backfill. Four small defects were found and repaired with the approved patch material. Each repair was retested at the specified voltage and the results were entered on the holiday log for Holly Sutherland to review.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The site welfare cabins are being connected to the mains water supply on Wednesday. The plumber will need access to the cabin roofs and the rear service duct. Please move any personal items away from the cabin walls and ensure the taps are closed when you leave, so the pressure test can be carried out without interruption.
