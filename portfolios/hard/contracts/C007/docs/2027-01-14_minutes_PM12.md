## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

54,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Grace Bryant will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 3. Progress

Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Mechanical - Install package skids; Area 300 Utilities - Mechanical - Align rotating equipment; Area 100 Inlet - Electrical - Pull cables (part 1 of 2); Area 400 Storage - Structural - Install grating and handrail.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 28 December 2027 against contract completion 09 July 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

Instrument datasheets for the flow transmitters on the gas compression package contain inconsistent process conditions. Two sheets show a line pressure of 42 barg, while the heat and mass balance uses 38 barg. The instrumentation engineer has raised an RFI to confirm the operating case and will update the affected datasheets once the process team responds.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 12 submitted on 08 January 2027.

BRK/CPD/Q/057: Hannah Fraser said he expected to be in a position to agree the quotation following the joint inspection on 08 December 2026.

## 7. Quality

Dimensional checks on the skid frame for the chemical dosing package were recorded against the approved fabrication drawings. Base plate levels were within tolerance, but the nozzle orientation on one vessel was rotated by about four degrees. The fabricator has been asked to submit a proposed correction for review by the design team.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The overhead line crossing the site entrance has been identified as a risk to tall vehicles. Height barriers will be installed at the approach and the warning signs repositioned. Drivers of tipper trucks and mobile cranes must check the clearance before entering, and any vehicle that cannot pass safely must be turned around at the lay-by.

## 9. Any other business

The commissioning team has requested a fortnightly review of the instrument calibration certificates for the water treatment plant. Several certificates from the supplier are still awaiting signature by the independent inspector. Claire Iqbal will maintain a tracker listing each tag number, the due date and the name of the person responsible for chasing the paperwork.
