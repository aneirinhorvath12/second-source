# Internal memo

**To:** Ian Talbot, Site Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Grace Bryant, Project Manager
**Date:** 16 November 2026
**Subject:** Document control procedure update, Revision C

The document control procedure for Corran Point Desalination has been revised to Revision C. It takes effect from Monday and replaces Revision B in full. Controlled copy holders will receive the new issue through the document management system, and paper copies held at the Corran Point, Argyll document hub will be swapped on the same day.

**Key changes**

- Every transmittal must quote the current document register number and revision letter. Transmittals without these will be returned unactioned.
- Superseded markups and redlines are to be moved to the site records store within two working days of a revised issue being received.
- Issued-for-construction drawings printed for the field must carry the stamp and be logged on the hub sheet before they leave the document room.
- Vendor documents for Area 200 Process are routed through the engineering team for review before issue to site, not sent direct.

**Actions**

1. Site managers to brief supervisors on the changes at Friday's coordination meeting.
2. Document control to remove Revision B copies from the field offices and confirm the sweep in writing.
3. Any questions on the new register format to the document controller before Friday.

Please make sure your teams know that the old transmittal template is withdrawn. Thank you for getting this done quickly.
