## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

45,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 6 near misses reported.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Grace Bryant has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Claire Iqbal will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 7. Quality

Commissioning certificates for the fire water pumps were checked against the test schedule. Flow and pressure results met the specified values and the controller tests were recorded. The original factory certificate was missing one page, so the vendor has been asked to send a complete copy for the handover file.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.
