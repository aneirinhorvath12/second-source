## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Ferris will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 39.4%. Activities progressed this period: Area 400 Storage - Civils - Backfill and compact; Area 200 Process - Civils - Pour foundations (part 2 of 2); Area 400 Storage - Civils - Excavate and blind (part 1 of 2); Area 300 Utilities - Structural - Erect secondary steel.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 15 April 2028 against contract completion 07 September 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

4 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

Protection relay settings for the 33 kV feeder were checked against the coordination study. The time grading between the incoming breaker and the outgoing feeders appears tight at one location. The protection engineer will rerun the study with the latest transformer data and issue a revised settings schedule for approval.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Document review of the as-built drawings for the water treatment plant continued this week. Several isometrics showed valves in the wrong position, and the markups have been passed to the drawing office. A revised set will be issued once the changes have been checked against the latest site photographs and the field notes.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The customer has asked for the sewer connection inspection to be booked before the drainage trench is backfilled. Daniel Sutherland has confirmed the inspector will attend on Friday at ten. Please leave the trench open and the pipework visible, and keep the access ladder in place so the inspector can view the joints safely.

## 9. Any other business

The noise survey for the water treatment plant will be repeated next week because some readings were taken during a period of heavy plant use. Crews are asked to keep the rotary drills and the diesel compressor off the plant between 10:00 and 11:30 on the survey days. The acoustic consultant will issue the timetable on Monday.
