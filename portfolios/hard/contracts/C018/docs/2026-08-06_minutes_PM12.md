# Pellow Wind Substation: progress meeting no. 12

**Date:** 06 August 2026, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Sophie Hale (ER), Daniel Sutherland, Dev Gallagher, Marcus Moffat (Ardent Water Ltd); Laura Ferris, Craig Ellison, Nadia Kerr, Neil Fraser (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

56,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

Noise surveys at the compressor enclosure showed that hearing protection signage needs refreshing in two places. Hearing protection is mandatory in the zone, but some visitors have entered without ear defenders. The gatehouse will check visitors for ear protection, and the signs will be replaced with clearer wording and pictograms. Monitoring will continue for the rest of the month.

Segregated walkways and vehicle routes have been reviewed around the new tanker bay. Delivery drivers were seen reversing without a banksman on two occasions last week. A second banksman will be rostered during peak delivery hours, and yellow lines have been repainted at the turning circle. Daniel Sutherland has been notified of the change to the logistics route through the customer gate.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 34.1%. Activities progressed this period: Area 200 Process - Civils - Install underground drainage; Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Pour foundations (part 2 of 2); Area 200 Process - Civils - Pour foundations (part 1 of 2); Area 100 Inlet - Civils - Backfill and compact (part 3 of 3).

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 15 April 2028 against contract completion 07 September 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

13 RFIs open, 10 closed in the period.

A request for information was raised by Kestrel Cranes this morning concerning the support spacing on the 150 mm carbon steel line running along the pipe rack in Area 400 Storage. The isometric shows supports at 3.0 m centres, whereas the stress report assumes 2.5 m. Chloe Achebe has asked the design team to confirm which basis governs and to issue a marked-up isometric before the next spool release meeting.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 12 submitted on 29 July 2026.

## 7. Quality

The mechanical completion walkdown for Area 100 Inlet identified sixteen punch list items, mostly relating to missing labels, damaged insulation and a leaking drain cap. Each item was photographed and assigned to a responsible subcontractor. Close-out will be verified by the quality team before the system is handed over to operations.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Nadia Kerr so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Vehicle movement near the crane lay down has been noted as too close to the exclusion zone on several occasions. Banksmen are reminded to hold their position at the edge of the marked boundary and not to step inside it while a lift is in progress. Dedicated signage will be placed at each corner by the end of the week.
