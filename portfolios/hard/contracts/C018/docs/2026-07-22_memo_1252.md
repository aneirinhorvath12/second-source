# Internal memo

**To:** Nadia Kerr, Site Manager, Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**From:** Dev Gallagher, Clerk of Works
**Date:** 22 July 2026
**Subject:** Archaeological watching brief update, week 11

This memo gives an update on the archaeological watching brief for the works within Area 600 Control Building this week. The brief is being carried out by the archaeologist from Kestrel Cranes under the agreed written scheme of investigation, and the county archaeologist has been kept informed throughout.

**Findings this week**

- Trench 4, on the eastern edge of the cable route, exposed a ditch running roughly north to south. The fill contained a small number of medieval pottery sherds and a fragment of animal bone.
- The finds have been bagged, labelled and recorded on the find sheet. They are held in the finds tray in the site office, and the tray must not be moved without the archaeologist's signature.
- No human remains have been found so far on the route.

**Working arrangements**

The exclusion zone around Trench 4 is to remain in place until the archaeologist has completed the recording, which is expected by Wednesday. Excavation for the drainage run in grid line C is to be monitored by the archaeologist on the day, and the machine operator should stop work immediately if any layer of dark soil or unusual material is exposed.

Piling on grid line C is planned for next week. The archaeologist will need to attend the first two piles, and the piling subcontractor should be told the start time in advance so that the attendance can be arranged.

**Actions**

- Nadia Kerr to brief the drainage gang on the stop-work requirement before Monday's start.
- The archaeologist to send the interim findings report to Daniel Sutherland by Friday.
- I will walk the route with Kestrel Cranes on Tuesday morning to confirm the revised boundary of the exclusion zone.
