39,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 400 Storage. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Dalgety Electrical has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Laura Ferris will sample the lifting records next week.
