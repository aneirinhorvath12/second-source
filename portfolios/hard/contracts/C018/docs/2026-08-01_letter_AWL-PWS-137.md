Dear Laura,

I am writing to remind you of the requirements for the quarterly carbon reporting return that Ardent Water Ltd must submit under its corporate environmental commitments. Your contribution to this return is due by the tenth working day of the month following the end of each quarter.

The return needs to cover the following for the Brackmoor scope on Pellow, Ceredigion:

- Fuel consumed by plant and generators, recorded in litres of red diesel and hours run per item of equipment
- Grid electricity drawn through the temporary supply, taken from the meter readings at the start and end of each month
- Gases used in welding and cutting, including the number and size of cylinders delivered and returned
- Waste removed from site, with the tonnage and the waste carrier's transfer note reference for each load
- Business travel by road and air for project staff, using expense records and fleet mileage logs

The figures should be reconciled against the delivery notes held in the site records. Where a figure is estimated, please state the basis of the estimate in the covering note. Our reviewers have queried estimates in previous returns and I would prefer to avoid this happening again.

Please send the completed data to Sophie Hale and copy me in. If you believe any of the data will not be available by the due date, let me know as early as possible so that we can agree the approach.
