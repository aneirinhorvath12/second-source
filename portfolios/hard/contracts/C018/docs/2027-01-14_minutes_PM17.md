# Pellow Wind Substation: progress meeting no. 17

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Sophie Hale (ER), Daniel Sutherland, Dev Gallagher, Marcus Moffat (Ardent Water Ltd); Laura Ferris, Craig Ellison, Nadia Kerr, Neil Fraser (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

25,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Gas testing of the sump chamber at the water treatment plant recorded oxygen at 20.8 per cent and no hydrocarbon readings above zero. Entry is permitted under the standby arrangement with a trained attendant at the top of the access. Rescue equipment has been checked and placed at the entry point. The attendant must not leave the post, and the entry log must be signed at each change of worker.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 49.3%. Activities progressed this period: Area 200 Process - Electrical - Install cable tray (part 2 of 2); Area 100 Inlet - Instrumentation - Install instruments; Area 200 Process - Piping - Install spools (part 3 of 3); Area 200 Process - Mechanical - Install package skids.

Productivity remains below plan; new supervision team mobilised.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 15 April 2028 against contract completion 07 September 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

6 RFIs open, 2 closed in the period.

Protection relay settings for the 33 kV feeder were checked against the coordination study. The time grading between the incoming breaker and the outgoing feeders appears tight at one location. The protection engineer will rerun the study with the latest transformer data and issue a revised settings schedule for approval.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Quality records for the pipe supports on Area 300 Utilities were audited against the drawing register. Several support drawings had been superseded without the earlier revision being withdrawn from site. The document controller has issued a list of affected drawings and the site team has been asked to remove obsolete copies from the work packs.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Nadia Kerr so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Operatives are reminded that the walkway is closed and that the alternative route via the service stair must be used. Any person found on the walkway during the closure will be removed from the site for the rest of the day.
