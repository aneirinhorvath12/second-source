The federated model for Area 100 Inlet was updated on Monday with the latest issue of the pipe routing and the revised cable tray layout from the electrical package. The clash run completed on Tuesday evening.

Current position:

- 412 clashes were reported in the latest run, down from 538 the previous week.
- Of these, 96 are hard clashes between pipework and structural steel, most of them on the north side of the compressor skid.
- 37 are marked as resolved in the model but remain open on the coordination log until the responsible discipline confirms.
- The remainder are soft clashes around insulation and access clearance, which will be reviewed at the coordination meeting on Thursday.
