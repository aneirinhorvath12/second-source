## 2. Health, safety and environment

49,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Laura Ferris will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Chloe Achebe has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

## 5. Design and technical

11 RFIs open, 6 closed in the period.

A technical query from Ridgeway Civils concerns the material specification for the gaskets on the sulphuric acid line. The drawing calls for a PTFE-based gasket, but the purchase specification lists a graphite option. Engineering has been asked to confirm the chemical compatibility and to issue a single approved specification to both teams.

## 7. Quality

Non-destructive testing reports for the pressure vessel nozzles were issued by the independent inspection body. Magnetic particle testing found no relevant indications on the attachment welds. The reports have been checked against the weld map and signed off by Dev Gallagher, so the vessel can move to the next stage.

Please move any personal items away from the cabin walls and ensure the taps are closed when you leave, so the pressure test can be carried out without interruption.
