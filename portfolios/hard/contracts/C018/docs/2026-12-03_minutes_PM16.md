## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Ardent Water Ltd representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

## 7. Quality

Supervisors should check their teams during the morning walk round and report any mismatch to the site safety adviser, who will keep the register updated.
