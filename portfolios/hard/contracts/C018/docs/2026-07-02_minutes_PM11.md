## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

42,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.

Harness inspection records for Kestrel Cranes show that eleven fall arrest harnesses were removed from service during the month because of frayed stitching or damaged buckles. Replacements have been issued and the old harnesses have been cut up and disposed of. Each worker must present their harness for a pre-use check before going onto the pipe rack, and supervisors will log the checks on the daily permit.

## 5. Design and technical

9 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

A design check of the sump pump sizing for the process area showed that the calculated flow rate is slightly higher than the figure on the datasheet. The mechanical engineer has asked the vendor to confirm whether the selected pump still meets the head requirement at the higher flow. A response is expected before the datasheet is updated.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 11 submitted on 26 June 2026.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Amira Carver before the joint was closed up.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Pipe supports for the new utility corridor are being stored on timber bearers in the north field. The bearers were checked for rot and the stacks have been tied off with straps. Please do not move any support without signing it out at the stores, as the bundle numbers are needed for the erection sequence.

## 9. Any other business

Crane mats for the lift on the compressor skid were found cracked at the edges during Monday's inspection. The damaged mats have been removed from the laydown area and tagged. Replacement mats are due on site next Tuesday. Laura Ferris asked that the lift plan be reissued once the new mats are in place, with the ground bearing calculation attached for the lifting supervisor's sign-off.
