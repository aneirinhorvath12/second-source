I observed the fire evacuation drill for Area 300 Utilities carried out last Thursday. The alarm was raised at 10:15 and the building was cleared in good order. The principal findings are set out below.

**Observations**

- Roll call was completed in 11 minutes and 40 seconds. The target is 10 minutes.
- Two contractors were not on the signing-in register and could not be accounted for at the assembly point until their names were checked against the gate log.
- The fire door in the compressor house was propped open with a wooden wedge. This would have compromised the compartment.
- The extinguisher at bay 4 was past its inspection date.
- Two of the four marshals were not wearing hi-vis and were not identifiable to the crowd.
- The assembly point at the north gate was clear and well signed.

**Actions requested**

Please arrange for the compressor house door to be fitted with a free-closing device and the extinguisher replaced before the next drill. Marshals should be issued with fresh hi-vis and briefed on their roles. The signing-in register should be checked against the gate log at the start of each shift.

I would be grateful for written confirmation of these actions by the end of the month, together with the date of the next drill, so that Ruth Varga can attend.
