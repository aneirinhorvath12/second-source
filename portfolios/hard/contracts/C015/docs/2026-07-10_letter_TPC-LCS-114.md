**Instruction**

You are instructed to provide an additional access road and turning head for the Employer's operations team at the Linford Chemical Storage site. The detail is as follows:

- The road is to leave the existing site access road at the north-east corner of the tank farm and run along the eastern boundary to a turning head adjacent to the operations building.
- The carriageway is to be 5.5 m wide with a surfaced turning head sized for a 16.5 m articulated vehicle, and is to be capable of carrying tanker traffic.
- Surface water drainage is to connect into the existing site drainage system, and lighting and kerbing are to match the existing access road.

Under clause 14.2, please submit a quotation for this instruction within 14 days of the date of this letter. The quotation should state any adjustment you consider necessary to the Contract Completion Date, together with the supporting programme information.

Please quote reference ERI-030 on the quotation and on any related correspondence. Setting-out information for the road alignment can be provided on request, and Ruth Varga will arrange a site meeting if you would find that helpful before you price the work.
