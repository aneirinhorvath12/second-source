## 2. Health, safety and environment

34,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.
