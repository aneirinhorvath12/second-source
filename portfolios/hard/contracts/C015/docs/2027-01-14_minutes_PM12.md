## 2. Health, safety and environment

33,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

Lock-out tag-out on the gas compression package was reviewed following a query about isolation certificates. Two certificates for the suction scrubber did not show the valve numbers as they appear on the P&ID. Operations have confirmed the correct isolation points and the certificates are being reissued. Tamsin Petrochemicals representatives will be invited to observe the next isolation so that the process can be witnessed.

The LOLER thorough examination of the 25 tonne mobile crane is due next Thursday, and the current certificate was checked at the meeting. The hook block and wire rope inspection records are complete, but the outrigger pad condition report is still outstanding from the hire company. Until it is received, the crane will only be used within the limits of the existing load chart, and lifts will be planned accordingly.
