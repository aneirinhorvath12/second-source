Hi Claire Kowalski,

Thank you for the time you took to walk the site with my team on Tuesday after the telehandler contact with the pipe rack. I have now completed our internal review and wanted to share the main points so they can go into the next toolbox talk.

What happened: the operator was reversing a loaded pallet out of the laydown bay and did not have a banksman in place. The reversing alarm was working, but the rear view was blocked by the load. The contact damaged one pipe support bracket, with no injuries and no leak.

What we have changed:
- A banksman is now mandatory for any reversing move in a congested area, regardless of distance
- Loads must be sited so the operator has a clear rear view before lifting
- The laydown bay has been re-marked with exclusion lines to keep foot traffic away from the reversing path
- Our daily plant checks now include a camera and mirror test

What we would like from the wider site: a short reminder to all plant operators in the next briefing, and a request to Bethan Thornton to add the bay marking to the weekly inspection checklist.

The damaged bracket has been reported to your team and I understand it has already been replaced.
