**Subject:** Draft content for the community liaison newsletter, issue 4

## Purpose

The next community liaison newsletter goes to residents, the parish council and local businesses on the route of the access road. It needs to be issued by the end of the month. The customer's community team has approved the format, so the content below is the draft for your review.

## Proposed content

**Progress on site**
The foundations for the new compressor building are complete. Steelwork erection will start in the next fortnight and will be visible from the public footpath on the north side of the site.

**Working hours and deliveries**
Deliveries are restricted to 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays. Night deliveries are not planned for this period. Any change will be notified in advance.

**Traffic management**
The temporary traffic lights on the B-road will be moved on the 14th. Diversion signs will be in place from the Monday before.

**Noise**
The piling work is finished. The remaining noisy activities are the concrete pours and the crane lifts. We will give residents at least 48 hours' notice of any night working.

**How to contact us**
The community helpline and the email address for complaints will be printed in the back page, along with the site office opening hours.

## Actions

Please check the traffic dates and the steelwork timing with the planner before the draft goes to the customer. Send any photographs for the progress section to me by Friday. I will then forward the full draft to Ruth Varga for comment.
