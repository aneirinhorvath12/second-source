## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

A reversing dumper almost struck a stored gas bottle rack at the laydown yard when its reversing alarm was found to be disconnected. The operator stopped when a banksman signalled, and no contact was made. The machine has been removed from service pending repair. All plant operators have been reminded that alarms and lights must work and that pre-start checks must be recorded in the logbook.

Actions from the last HSE walk round have been closed except for three items: barrier replacement at the pipe rack, updated signage at the gas detection zone, and fire blanket checks in the welding bay. Owners have confirmed dates for all three, and Dev Nair will report completion at the next progress meeting. Thanks are extended to all crews who reported hazards during the month.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Fire hydrant access on the east side of the unit must stay clear at all times. Two pallets of valves were found blocking the hydrant on Tuesday, and the fire officer has asked for the area to be signed. Please keep the route to the hydrant open so that a fire tender can reach it within two minutes of an alarm.

The electrical foreman has listed the affected positions on a marked-up layout and will rectify them on the afternoon shift. A second check will be made by the independent electrical inspector before the containment is closed up.
