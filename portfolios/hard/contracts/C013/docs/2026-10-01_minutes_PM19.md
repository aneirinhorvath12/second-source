## 2. Health, safety and environment

58,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Ruth Iqbal will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

The scaffold on the south side of the separator vessel was inspected and tagged green following the recent modifications. The inspector noted that a toe board was missing on one lift and a ladder access was positioned incorrectly. Both points were corrected before the tag was signed. Users should check the tag before access and report any damage or loose fittings immediately to the scaffold foreman.
