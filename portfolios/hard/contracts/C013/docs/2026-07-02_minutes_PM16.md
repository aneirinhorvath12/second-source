## 2. Health, safety and environment

47,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

A small hydraulic oil leak from a telehandler was contained within the drip tray at the jetty laydown yard. The machine was taken out of service, the contaminated absorbent was bagged and placed in the correct waste stream, and the ground has been cleaned. Plant owners have been reminded that all mobile plant must be checked for leaks before leaving the compound each morning and that drip trays must stay in place.

Safety observation cards submitted this month totalled 84, of which 61 were positive recognitions and 23 flagged unsafe acts. The most common unsafe acts involved people standing under suspended loads and working without eye protection during grinding. Feedback has been given to the individuals concerned, and the common themes will be covered at next week's toolbox talks.
