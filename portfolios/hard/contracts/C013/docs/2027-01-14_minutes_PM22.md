## 2. Health, safety and environment

27,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Kieran Rowe will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

Permit documents for the switchroom isolation have been checked against the single line diagram. The earthing points listed on the permit did not match the points marked on site, so the permit has been returned to the authorised person for correction. No work on live equipment took place. Ruth Iqbal will attend the next isolation and sign the earthing record before any worker enters the cordoned area.
