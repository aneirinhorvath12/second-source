## 2. Health, safety and environment

25,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

A reversing dumper almost struck a stored gas bottle rack at the laydown yard when its reversing alarm was found to be disconnected. The operator stopped when a banksman signalled, and no contact was made. The machine has been removed from service pending repair. All plant operators have been reminded that alarms and lights must work and that pre-start checks must be recorded in the logbook.

A stock check of personal protective equipment in the stores found adequate supplies of hard hats and safety boots but a shortage of flame-retardant overalls for the welding team. An urgent replacement has been arranged for the required sizes. Until delivery, welders must work only in their existing undamaged overalls, and supervisors will check that clothing is free from oil contamination before welding begins.

## 7. Quality

The coating holiday test on the buried pipeline section was carried out at the trench bottom before backfill. Four small defects were found and repaired with the approved patch material. Rhys Hale will attend the pre-start meeting to confirm the isolation points. Lighting towers are to be positioned along the access road to give safe walking routes to the tie-in location.

## 9. Any other business

Heavy rain over the weekend left standing water in the pipe rack corridor and on the access road beyond the second gate. Plant operators have been asked to check the surface before driving heavy equipment across. Temporary sump pumps will be relocated to the lowest point by the drainage contractor, and the site team will report any further flooding to the weekly safety forum.
