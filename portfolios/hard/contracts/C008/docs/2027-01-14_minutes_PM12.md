# Ashby Fell Pumping Station: progress meeting no. 12

**Date:** 14 January 2027, 10:00, site offices  
**Present:** Marcus Walsh (ER), Bethan Patel, Hannah Yates, Craig Sinclair (Hexley Chemicals); Kieran Thornton, Priya Varga, Rhys Pemberton, Ade Doyle (Brackmoor Engineering Ltd)  
**Apologies:** none

## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

23,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 2 near misses reported.

Waste segregation has improved across the site since the new skips were labelled to match the local waste contractor's codes. However, mixed general waste was found in the hazardous skip at the north gate. The skip has been emptied and checked, and the labels have been re-fixed. Team leaders are asked to brief their crews on what can and cannot go into each container.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Dev Hale has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

## 3. Progress

Overall physical progress 37.0%. Activities progressed this period: Area 100 Inlet - Electrical - Install cable tray; Area 600 Control Building - Civils - Install underground drainage (part 2 of 3); Area 600 Control Building - Civils - Backfill and compact; Area 400 Storage - Structural - Erect secondary steel.

## 4. Programme

Programme update issued. Forecast completion 09 May 2028 against contract completion 23 August 2028.

## 5. Design and technical

3 RFIs open, 7 closed in the period.

Revision C of the foundation drawing for the compressor skid was issued to site yesterday. The main change is the anchor bolt pattern, which moved to suit the revised baseplate supplied by the vendor. Site engineers have checked the new layout against the setting-out survey and flagged two bolts that clash with an existing embedded plate. Hannah Yates will attend the next walkdown to confirm the resolution.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

Application for payment no. 12 submitted on 11 January 2027.

## 7. Quality

Visual inspection of the stainless steel vessel internals was carried out before the manway covers were refitted. The inspector found a small amount of weld spatter on a support ring and recorded it on the inspection report. Cleaning and passivation of the affected area will be witnessed before the vessel is closed.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Crane access to the tank farm is restricted by the overhead cable run near the bund wall. The lifting supervisor has marked the swing radius with yellow paint and fitted a goal post to protect the cables. All lifts over this line must be planned on the lift plan and signed off by the duty engineer before the crane is moved.

## 9. Any other business

A reminder that all hot work permits must be countersigned by the area authority before the grinder or welding set is brought into Area 300 Utilities. Several permits last week were found to be missing the gas test record. Permit holders should bring their own gas detector to the morning briefing, and fire watch cover must continue for thirty minutes after the final task has finished.
