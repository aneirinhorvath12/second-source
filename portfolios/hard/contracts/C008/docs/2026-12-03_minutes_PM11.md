## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

28,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

The lifting plan for the 14 tonne column placement was approved this week after review by the lift supervisor. Ground bearing checks were completed and the crane mats were laid out as specified. Exclusion zones will be set up with barriers and signage on the day, and only the appointed signaller will give instructions to the crane operator during the lift.

This month's slips, trips and falls campaign focuses on cable trays, hoses and uneven ground near the tank farm bund walls. Everyone is asked to record trip hazards on the daily walk round card rather than waiting for the weekly inspection. Boot cleaning stations have been placed at two gates to reduce mud carried onto the gravel roads, and Hannah Yates will track the number of reports raised over the next four weeks.

## 5. Design and technical

11 RFIs open, 9 closed in the period.

Protection relay settings for the 33 kV feeder were checked against the coordination study. The time grading between the incoming breaker and the outgoing feeders appears tight at one location. The protection engineer will rerun the study with the latest transformer data and issue a revised settings schedule for approval.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

Radiographic examination of the 6-inch process pipework welds on Area 300 Utilities was completed this morning by Tregarth Groundworks. Two welds returned indications within acceptance limits and one required a repair grind. The film package has been logged against the isometric drawings and passed to Hannah Yates for review before the repair is carried out.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The contractor's car park has been moved to the field beyond the security fence. A minibus will run every fifteen minutes from the hardstanding at the roundabout between six and eighteen hundred. Anyone who needs to park closer for medical or mobility reasons should speak to Rhys Pemberton so a bay can be arranged at the gatehouse.

## 9. Any other business

Cable tray supports on the north side of Area 300 Utilities are missing their earthing straps in several locations. The electrical foreman has listed the affected positions on a marked-up layout and will rectify them on the afternoon shift. A second check will be made by the independent electrical inspector before the containment is closed up.
