**Subject:** Procurement status: materials on site

This note records where we stand on the materials we bought early, so that the three of us are working from the same figure when we look at the month-end cost report.

**Position**

We bought the structural steel, the pipe spools and the valves early, to secure the prices we had. The value of those materials now on site or in the bonded store, and not yet installed, is £4,834,000. The steel is mostly in the laydown area by the north gate, the spools are split between the laydown area and the store, and the valves are all in the bonded store.

**How this shows in the reports**

- **Cost to date.** The materials are costed to the job on receipt, so cost to date already includes the full £4,834,000. Nothing further will be booked for them when they are fixed.
- **Physical progress.** Progress, and therefore earned value, only counts the materials once they are installed. Until then they sit in cost but not in progress. Anyone comparing cost to date against earned value will see a gap that is accounted for by this stock, and the gap will close as installation proceeds.
- **Estimate to complete.** The remaining budget in the ETC has been reduced by the same amount, because these items are already paid for. The ETC therefore carries no further cost for buying them, only the labour and plant to install them.
