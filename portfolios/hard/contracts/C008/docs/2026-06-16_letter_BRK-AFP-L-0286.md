We write to confirm our plans for the tie-in works on the pipe rack at Area 500 Export. The works require the tie-in of the new utility line to the existing header, which can only be completed with the line depressurised and isolated during a period of reduced activity. For this reason we propose to carry out the work at night over three consecutive nights.

The proposed working hours are 20:00 to 06:00, starting on the Thursday of next week. Lighting will be directed downwards and shielded to limit spill towards the residential properties on Lower Road. Reversing alarms on plant will be replaced with broadband or white noise alarms where the equipment supplier can provide them, and the generator will be positioned on the far side of the compound from the road.

Deliveries will be restricted to between 07:00 and 19:00, and no vehicles will be permitted to queue on the public highway. A banksman will be positioned at the compound gate throughout the night shifts.

A notification letter has been prepared for delivery to the neighbouring properties on Lower Road and Chapel Street. The draft text is attached for your review. It gives the dates and hours of the work, the reason for it, and a contact number for the site office, which will be staffed during working hours. A dedicated number will be available for complaints outside those hours and is also included in the notice.

Please let us know by Wednesday if you would like any changes to the wording before the notices are printed and delivered.
