**Subject:** PPE standards reminder, Tregarth Groundworks crews on Ashby Fell, Cumbria

Thanks for the reminder on PPE standards. We have gone through the requirements with all supervisors and the operatives who started on site this week, and I can confirm the following.

- Hi-visibility clothing to EN ISO 20471 is issued to every operative and is worn at all times on the works.
- Safety footwear to EN ISO 20345 is in use, and the foreman is checking boots at the start of each shift.
- Flame-retardant overalls are being worn for hot work and for any work near the process area, as set out in the permit conditions.
- Eye protection to EN 166 is worn for grinding, cutting and any work involving chemicals, and spare lenses are kept in the stores container.
- Hearing protection is worn in the posted noise zones, and the zones are marked on the access board.
- Gloves are chosen to suit the task, and the glove selection chart is displayed at the toolbox talk point.

We held a short toolbox talk on Monday covering the same points, and the attendance sheet has been sent to the site office. Any operative found without the correct PPE is being sent off the task until it is put right, and we are logging each instance in our own record so that we can see if anything is recurring.

If you spot anything on your rounds that you think we should address, please let me know directly and I will deal with it the same day. We are keen to keep this running smoothly for the rest of the phase.
