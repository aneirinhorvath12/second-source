**Subject:** Waste transfer notes and skip segregation

Following the site walk on Monday, I want to confirm how Dalgety Electrical will handle waste on Ashby Fell, Cumbria so that the transfer notes match what is actually going into each skip.

We are providing four skips, labelled and colour coded:

- Mixed construction waste (general skip)
- Clean, untreated timber only
- Metals
- Hazardous waste, such as contaminated soil or oil-filled items, goes in the sealed container at the compound and never in a skip.

Transfer notes:

- Each collection will be accompanied by a signed transfer note listing the waste code and the estimated tonnage.
- Skips must not be filled above the rim.
- Foremen to sign the daily skip check sheet by 16:00.

Please make sure the foremen in Area 600 Control Building understand the skip labels and point out anyone mixing timber or metals into general waste. If a load is found to be contaminated, we will arrange its removal the same day and let you know which skip it came from.
