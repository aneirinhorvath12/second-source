## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

44,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 3 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Rhys Pemberton will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 300 Utilities. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Tregarth Groundworks has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Kieran Thornton will sample the lifting records next week.

## 7. Quality

The daily quality walkdown on the jetty headstock identified missing identification tags on four valve actuators. Tags were replaced from the vendor kit and the tag register updated. Bethan Patel asked that a photograph of each tag be attached to the handover dossier so the operations team can match them to the loop drawings.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The reactor vessel is due on Thursday via the northern gate on a fourteen-axle trailer. The route survey and police escort were confirmed by Kieran Thornton last week. The haulier must reverse into laydown bay B3 with a banksman and a second spotter present. Rhys Pemberton will brief the lifting team before the vehicle enters the public highway.

## 9. Any other business

Smoking area signage at the main gate has been damaged by vehicles reversing into the post. A replacement sign has been ordered and the post will be re-set in concrete. In the meantime, a temporary sign has been put up on the fence. Supervisors are asked to remind their gangs of the designated areas during toolbox talks.
