## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

43,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 0 near misses reported.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Simon McLeod has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

An audit of permits to work over the past fortnight found three hot work permits where the fire watch had not signed the closing section. In each case the work was finished and the area left safe, but the paperwork was incomplete. Permit issuers have been reminded that the fire watch must remain for at least thirty minutes after welding or cutting stops. Kieran Thornton has asked for the permit board to be reviewed at the next safety forum.
