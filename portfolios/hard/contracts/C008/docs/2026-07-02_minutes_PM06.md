## 2. Health, safety and environment

30,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 4 near misses reported.

Dust monitoring during grinding and abrasive blasting near the site office recorded readings within the agreed limits, but two readings near the boundary fence were close to the upper threshold. Wetting of the work area and local extraction will continue. The team will reposition the blasting shroud and check the wind direction before starting work each morning to limit dust reaching neighbouring properties.

Summer temperatures forecast for next week mean the heat stress plan will be activated. Cool water stations will be checked twice daily at the substation compound, and shaded rest areas will be set up near the pipe bending area. Supervisors should watch for signs of heat exhaustion and encourage regular breaks, particularly for those wearing full chemical suits for painting.

The corrosion engineer is checking whether the difference reflects a later soil resistivity survey, and will confirm which version should be used for installation.

## 7. Quality

Torque verification on the bolted flanges for the tank farm inlet manifold was completed using a calibrated wrench. Each bolt was re-torqued in the star pattern and the values recorded on the torque sheet. Two flanges required a second pass after the gasket relaxed, and both were signed off by Simon McLeod before the joint was closed up.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The customer's security team has reviewed the new access card process for the control room extension. Each contractor operative must now present a valid card at the turnstile and sign the controlled-area register. Escorted visitors will be issued with a red lanyard, and Hannah Yates will check the register at the end of every working day.

## 9. Any other business

The gas detection system in Area 300 Utilities has been generating nuisance alarms during the morning cleaning rounds. Engineers suspect a fault in one of the portable reference units rather than a real leak. The unit has been removed for bench checking and a spare has been issued. Operators must still respond to every alarm as normal until the cause is confirmed.
