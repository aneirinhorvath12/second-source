## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

58,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

A trench for the new drainage run beside the pumphouse was found with a spoil heap less than a metre from the edge. The trench is more than 1.2 metres deep and there was no ladder within the required distance of the workers. Excavation was stopped until the spoil was moved back and a ladder was installed. Joanna Varga will ensure that trench inspection records are signed before work resumes each day.

The scaffold on the south side of the separator vessel was inspected and tagged green following the recent modifications. The inspector noted that a toe board was missing on one lift and a ladder access was positioned incorrectly. Both points were corrected before the tag was signed. Users should check the tag before access and report any damage or loose fittings immediately to the scaffold foreman.

## 5. Design and technical

14 RFIs open, 10 closed in the period.

A design check of the sump pump sizing for the process area showed that the calculated flow rate is slightly higher than the figure on the datasheet. The mechanical engineer has asked the vendor to confirm whether the selected pump still meets the head requirement at the higher flow. A response is expected before the datasheet is updated.

## 6. Commercial and contractual

## 7. Quality

The welding procedure specification for the duplex stainless steel pipework was reissued following a change of filler metal supplier. Welders were briefed on the revised interpass temperature limits and the new procedure was posted at each welding station. Old copies were collected and destroyed to avoid confusion.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The insulated pipe spools for Area 600 Control Building are arriving in six separate loads over the next three days. Each load needs a pre-booked slot at the goods reception cabin. Drivers must bring the delivery note, the material certificate copy and the photo ID that matches the booking. Late arrivals will be held at the weighbridge until a slot is free.

## 9. Any other business

Waste segregation at the laydown yard is still not meeting the agreed standard. Timber offcuts, metal cuttings and oily rags are being placed in the same skip. A separate skip for each waste stream has been ordered and will arrive on Monday. Until then, foremen are asked to walk their gangs through the labelled bins at the start of each shift.
