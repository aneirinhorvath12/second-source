## 1. Previous minutes

Agreed as a true record. Actions reviewed; see action log.

## 2. Health, safety and environment

23,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 5 near misses reported.

Reports of long shifts and fatigue have been reviewed following the increase in weekend working. The rota for the next four weeks has been checked, and no individual is scheduled for more than sixty hours in any seven-day period. Supervisors must watch for signs of tiredness during night shifts and allow rest breaks. Drivers of site vehicles must not drive if they feel unwell or drowsy.

A contractor's operative received a laceration to the palm while handling a sharp-edged flange gasket in the pipework area. First aid was given on site and the worker returned to light duties the same day. The operative was not wearing cut-resistant gloves, and the glove issue for that gang has been reviewed. Simon Davies has agreed to introduce a glove check at the start of each shift.

## 7. Quality

Radiographic examination of the 6-inch process pipework welds on Area 400 Storage was completed this morning by Pennant Instrumentation. Two welds returned indications within acceptance limits and one required a repair grind. The film package has been logged against the isometric drawings and passed to James Morgan for review before the repair is carried out.

Until then, the site office phone lines are down, so please use the mobile numbers on the noticeboard. Email access remains available through the portable satellite router.

## 9. Any other business

The noise survey for the water treatment plant will be repeated next week because some readings were taken during a period of heavy plant use. Crews are asked to keep the rotary drills and the diesel compressor off the plant between 10:00 and 11:30 on the survey days. The acoustic consultant will issue the timetable on Monday.
