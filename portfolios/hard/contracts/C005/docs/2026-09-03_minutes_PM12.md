## 1. Previous minutes

## 2. Health, safety and environment

21,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

COSHH assessments for the new epoxy coatings have been reviewed, and the data sheets are now available at the paint store. Two containers were found without hazard labels in the coatings store. Labels have been replaced and the chemical register updated. Painters must wear the specified respirators and gloves at all times and must not decant chemicals into unlabelled bottles.

Surface water discharge from the attenuation pond was sampled on Monday and sent for laboratory analysis. Results are awaited, and the pond outlet valve remains closed until they are received. Silt fences along the access road have been inspected and two sections need repair after heavy rain. Joanna Varga has asked the drainage team to clear the gully gratings before the next forecast rain.

Material traceability for the carbon steel flanges was reviewed in the stores office. Heat numbers on several boxes were partly illegible due to weather exposure. The storekeeper transferred the information from the mill certificates, marked the boxes again and added a note to the stock record.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

Batching plant drivers must use the designated wash-out area and must not discharge into the surface water drains. The banksman will guide each mixer into position, and the site engineer will sign the delivery tickets once the slump has been checked.
