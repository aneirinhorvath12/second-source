## 2. Health, safety and environment

30,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

A near miss was reported on Tuesday when a section of scaffold tube slipped from a bundle being lifted over the pipe rack in Area 600 Control Building. The banksman stopped the lift and nobody was beneath the load. Hallam Steel Erectors has been asked to review slinging arrangements for scaffold materials and to brief all crews before the next lift. The incident has been added to the register and Ravi Kowalski will sample the lifting records next week.

A spill response drill in the tank farm simulated a diesel release from a damaged transfer hose. The team deployed booms within eleven minutes, and the drain valves were closed as planned. Some confusion arose over who should contact the environment officer, so the call-out list at the bund gate will be updated. A debrief with all shift leaders is scheduled for Friday morning.
