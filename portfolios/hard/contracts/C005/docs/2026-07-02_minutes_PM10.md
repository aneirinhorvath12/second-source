## 2. Health, safety and environment

19,000 hours worked in the period; no RIDDOR reportable incidents; 1 near misses reported.

The hand and finger safety campaign this fortnight highlights pinch points on hand tools, pipe clamps and the chain blocks used on the skid frames. Short toolbox talks will be given at the start of each shift, and workers are encouraged to help identify pinch hazards in their work areas. Several gang leaders have volunteered to lead the talks, and Pennant Instrumentation will provide feedback at the next meeting.

A spill response drill in the tank farm simulated a diesel release from a damaged transfer hose. The team deployed booms within eleven minutes, and the drain valves were closed as planned. Some confusion arose over who should contact the environment officer, so the call-out list at the bund gate will be updated. A debrief with all shift leaders is scheduled for Friday morning.

## 7. Quality

Positive material identification was carried out on twelve spool pieces delivered to laydown at Tarrow, Tyne and Wear. Eleven matched the material certificates supplied by the vendor. One spool showed a chromium reading lower than expected and has been quarantined in the stores compound pending a retest by an independent laboratory.

## 8. Logistics and interfaces

The temporary power supply to the welfare compound will be switched over on Monday afternoon. The distribution board was tested by the electrician last Friday and the earth continuity readings were recorded. Anyone working on the site office kettles, heaters or chargers should unplug them before the changeover at fourteen hundred hours.

## 9. Any other business

The gas detection system in Area 500 Export has been generating nuisance alarms during the morning cleaning rounds. Engineers suspect a fault in one of the portable reference units rather than a real leak. The unit has been removed for bench checking and a spare has been issued. Operators must still respond to every alarm as normal until the cause is confirmed.
