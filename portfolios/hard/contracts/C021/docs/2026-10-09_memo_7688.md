EC-02 is attached in full in the engineering file. This note summarises it for the forecast.

## Basis

Tail-end SCR on each Line, downstream of the bag filters, with gas-gas reheat and a steam trim heater; new induced draught fans; new ductwork from the bag filter outlets to the stacks; strengthening of the FGT hall roof steel; electrical, control and CEMS integration. Installation split across the Planned Outages in 2028 and 2029, so that both Lines comply by 31 December 2029.

The estimate is concept level (expected accuracy −20% to +35%). It is priced at current rates with no allowance for the efficiency of combining the work with the existing Phases.

| **Total** | **£18,400,000** |

Range: low £15,200,000, most likely £18,400,000, high £24,500,000.

## What drives the range

The high case assumes the duct runs to the stacks have to be replaced rather than modified, and that the hall steel needs a new frame rather than local strengthening. We will not know either until the ducts and steel are opened up and tested. The Line 1 outage in April 2027 is the first opportunity; Line 2 follows in September 2027.

I would not expect the range to narrow much before the Line 1 survey results are in, probably June 2027.
