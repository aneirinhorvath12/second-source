# Internal memo

**To:** Martin Keale, Programme Director
**From:** Rhys Gethin, Site and Outage Manager
**Date:** 19 March 2026
**Subject:** Near-miss report: dropped bolt, Line 1 boiler second pass

**Date and time:** Wednesday 18 March, 15:20.

**What happened:** A flange bolt fell about 6 metres from the second-pass working platform onto the lower landing. Nobody was below; the landing was within the exclusion zone.

**Immediate actions:** Work on the platform stopped; tool tethering checked; loose items removed from the platform edge.

**Proposed actions:** Bolt bags to be used for all flange work at height; daily platform edge check by the Morven supervisor.
