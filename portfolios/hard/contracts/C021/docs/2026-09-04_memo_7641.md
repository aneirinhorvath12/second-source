# Internal memo: legally privileged

**To:** Fiona Ashdown, Commercial Director
**Cc:** Martin Keale, Programme Director
**From:** Sarah Linden, Legal Counsel
**Date:** 04 September 2026
**Subject:** Carnbrook: emission limits and clause 21

Fiona,

You asked whether Brackmoor can recover the cost of the work needed to meet the revised emission limits.

**The change is a General Change in Law.** The permit variation applies limits that apply to all energy recovery facilities. It is not a Discriminatory Change in Law, because it does not apply expressly to Carnbrook. It is not a Specific Change in Law, because it concerns the operation of facilities, not the provision of engineering or construction services. Clause 21.3 says so in terms for a change to the emission limits that apply to facilities generally.

**Consequence.** Under clause 21.2 Brackmoor bears the cost and has no entitlement to an adjustment of the Contract Price or the time for completion. Clause 13.3 confirms that the compliance work forms part of the Works.

**Notification.** Martin's letter BRK/CRN/L/0164 notified the event under clause 15.1 and reserved our rights. That is sensible, but I would not expect it to lead to any recovery.

**Recommendation.** We should not include any recovery in the forecast. There may be scope to discuss the sequencing of the work with the Employer, since the Employer controls the Planned Outages, but that is a programme discussion and not an entitlement.

Sarah
