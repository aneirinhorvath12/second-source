## Movement since the tender

Margin at tender was 9.5%. The movement is driven by the emissions compliance works required by the revised permit limits (EC-02, most likely value £18.4m), which are at Brackmoor's risk under clause 21.2, partly offset by programme efficiencies of £7.9m.

## Programme efficiencies

The efficiency programme covers value engineering, procurement and outage delivery across Phases 4 to 19. Workstreams are listed in the programme efficiency tracker. £7.9m is a stretch target for the remaining phases.

## FY27 milestones

- Phase 4 (Line 1, April to May 2027), including the intrusive emissions survey.
- Phase 5 (Line 2, September to October 2027), including the Line 2 survey.
- Confirmation of the emissions compliance scope and estimate after the Line 1 survey.
