# Programme Works Contract

**Project:** Carnbrook Energy Recovery Facility: Life Extension Programme
**Contract reference:** CRN-PWC-001
**Employer:** Harrowfield Energy Recovery Ltd
**Contractor:** Brackmoor Engineering Ltd
**Form:** Lump-sum fixed-price programme works agreement with bespoke Conditions
**Date of agreement:** 12 November 2024

## Articles of agreement

This agreement is made between Harrowfield Energy Recovery Ltd (the "Employer") and Brackmoor Engineering Ltd (the
"Contractor"). The Contractor shall design, procure, install, test and commission the Works in a series of Phases
during the Planned Outages of the Facility, in accordance with the Contract, and the Employer shall pay the Contractor
the Contract Price at the times and in the manner set out in the Contract.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

**Contract** means this agreement, the Conditions, the Employer's Requirements, the Contractor's Proposals and the Schedules, read together as one document.
**Contract Price** means the sum stated in clause 4.1, as adjusted in accordance with the Contract.
**Employer's Representative** means David Brindle, or such other person as the Employer notifies to the Contractor in writing.
**Facility** means the energy recovery facility at Carnbrook, comprising two combustion lines (Line 1 and Line 2), the turbine hall, the flue gas treatment plant for each Line, and the associated buildings and services.
**Line** means either of the two combustion lines of the Facility, each with its own boiler, flue gas treatment plant and stack flue.
**Planned Outage** means a period during which a Line is taken out of service for the Works, as set out in Schedule 2 or as otherwise agreed under clause 8.
**Phase** means the part of the Works to be carried out in a Planned Outage, as described in Schedule 2.
**Permit** means the environmental permit under which the Facility is operated, as varied, replaced or reissued from time to time.
**Applicable Law** means any law, regulation, statutory guidance or condition of any consent or permit, including the Permit, in force in England from time to time.
**Change in Law** means the coming into effect after the date of this agreement of any Applicable Law, or any change to an existing Applicable Law, including any variation of the Permit.
**Discriminatory Change in Law** means a Change in Law that applies expressly to the Facility and not to other energy recovery facilities, or that applies expressly to the Contractor and not to other contractors.
**Specific Change in Law** means a Change in Law that refers specifically to the provision of engineering, construction or maintenance services of the kind provided under this Contract, and not to the operation of facilities generally.
**General Change in Law** means a Change in Law that is neither a Discriminatory Change in Law nor a Specific Change in Law.
**Variation** means a change to the Works instructed or agreed under clause 14.
**Works** means the works described in Schedule 1, including all temporary works, and all work needed to meet the requirements of clause 2.5.
**Site** means the land at Carnbrook shown edged red on drawing CRN-GA-0001, together with any part of the Facility made available to the Contractor for a Planned Outage.
**Completion** means the stage at which all Phases are complete, save for minor defects, and the Facility has passed the Tests on Completion in Schedule 3.
**Programme** means the Contractor's programme accepted under clause 8.2, as revised.
**Working Day** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

## 2. Contractor's general obligations

2.1 The Contractor shall carry out and complete the Works with the skill, care and diligence of a
competent contractor experienced in works of a similar size, scope and complexity on operating energy
recovery facilities.

2.2 The Contractor shall comply with all Applicable Law and the Employer's site rules, including the
Employer's permit-to-work system.

2.3 The Contractor is responsible for the design of the Works, including the development and
completion of the design from the Employer's Requirements. No development, completion or correction
of the Contractor's design, and no work needed to meet the Employer's Requirements, shall constitute
a Variation or entitle the Contractor to any adjustment of the Contract Price.

2.4 The Contractor shall be deemed to have inspected the Facility and satisfied itself as to its
condition so far as reasonably ascertainable from the condition reports in Schedule 5 and a visual
inspection of those parts of the Facility accessible during normal operation.

2.5 On the completion of each Phase, and at Completion, the Works shall enable each Line to be operated
in compliance with the Permit as then in force, and the Works shall include all plant, equipment and
modifications needed for that purpose.

## 3. Employer's Representative

3.1 The Employer appoints the Employer's Representative to act on its behalf under the Contract.

3.2 Only the Employer's Representative may issue instructions, instruct or agree Variations, or agree
any adjustment to the Contract Price. No other employee or agent of the Employer, including the Plant
Manager and the shift managers, has authority to instruct or agree any change to the Works.

3.3 If the Employer's Representative gives an instruction other than in writing, the Contractor shall
confirm it in writing to the Employer's Representative within 2 Working Days. If the Employer's
Representative does not dissent in writing within 7 days of receiving the confirmation, the
instruction shall take effect from the expiry of that period.

## 4. Contract Price and payment

4.1 The Contract Price is £146,200,000, fixed and not subject to adjustment except as expressly
provided in the Contract.

4.2 All sums stated in or under the Contract are exclusive of VAT, which shall be added at the
applicable rate.

4.3 The Contract Price is not subject to indexation. The Contractor has allowed in the Contract Price
for all changes in the cost of labour, plant and materials over the duration of the Works.

## 8. Programme, Planned Outages and time for completion

8.1 The Contractor shall commence the Works on 06 January 2025 and proceed regularly and diligently.

8.2 The Contractor shall submit a programme within 28 days. The Contractor shall submit a revised
programme each month showing actual progress and forecast completion, and a detailed outage plan for
each Phase no later than 12 weeks before the start of its Planned Outage.

8.3 The Planned Outages are as set out in Schedule 2. The Employer may, on not less than 6 months'
notice, move a Planned Outage by up to 8 weeks to suit the operation of the Facility. The Contractor
shall not be entitled to any adjustment of the Contract Price for such a move.

8.4 The Contractor shall return each Line to the Employer, available for service, by the last day of
its Planned Outage.

8.5 The Contractor shall achieve Completion by 15 December 2034.

8.6 If the Contractor fails to return a Line by the last day of its Planned Outage, or fails to achieve
Completion by the date in clause 8.5, it shall pay the Employer delay damages of £48,000 per day of
delay, up to a maximum aggregate liability of £7,310,000 over the duration of the Contract.

## 13. Compliance with Applicable Law and the Permit

13.1 The Contractor shall carry out the Works so that the Facility, and each Line returned to the
Employer, complies with Applicable Law.

13.2 The Employer shall give the Contractor a copy of the Permit and of any notice of variation of the
Permit within 10 Working Days of receiving it.

13.3 Work needed for the Facility to comply with the Permit, including work needed because the Permit
is varied after the date of this agreement, forms part of the Works. Clause 21 alone determines whether
the Contractor is entitled to any adjustment of the Contract Price in respect of such work.

## 14. Variations

14.1 No change to the Works shall constitute a Variation unless instructed or agreed in writing by the
Employer's Representative.

14.2 Before carrying out a Variation, or within 14 days of an instruction, the Contractor shall
submit a quotation stating the proposed adjustment to the Contract Price and any effect on the
Programme or the Planned Outages.

14.3 The Employer's Representative shall, within 28 days of receiving a quotation, accept it, accept it
in part, or reject it. A quotation accepted in part is accepted only to the extent stated.

14.4 Failing agreement, the value of a Variation shall be determined under clause 20.

14.5 The Employer's instructions are issued with an Employer's Instruction (EI) number. The Contractor
may register an instruction under its own Variation reference, but the Employer is not bound by the
Contractor's numbering.

## 15. Notification of events

15.1 The Contractor shall notify the Employer's Representative of any event that it considers entitles
it to an adjustment of the Contract Price or the time for completion within 8 weeks of becoming aware
of the event.

15.2 If the Contractor does not notify an event within the period in clause 15.1, it is not entitled to
any adjustment of the Contract Price or the time for completion in respect of that event.

## 16. Payment

16.1 The Contractor shall submit an application for payment at the end of each month, valued in
accordance with the payment schedule in Schedule 6. The Employer's Representative shall issue an
interim payment certificate within 14 days.

16.2 An interim payment certificate is not evidence that any work, or the value of any Variation, has
been accepted or agreed.

## 17. Insurance

17.1 The Contractor shall maintain contractor's all risks insurance for the full reinstatement value
of the Works, and employer's and public liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 per event.
The Employer maintains material damage and business interruption insurance for the Facility.

## 18. Health, safety and environment

18.1 The Contractor is the principal contractor for the purposes of the Construction (Design and
Management) Regulations 2015 for each Phase and shall comply with the Employer's site HSE rules and
permit-to-work system.

## 19. Quality

19.1 The Contractor shall operate a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 and shall give
the Employer's Representative 5 Working Days' notice of each witness and hold point.

## 20. Disputes

20.1 Either party may refer a dispute to adjudication at any time. The adjudicator's decision is
binding until finally determined by agreement or by the courts of England and Wales.

## 21. Change in Law

21.1 If a Discriminatory Change in Law or a Specific Change in Law increases the cost to the Contractor
of carrying out the Works, the Contractor shall be entitled to an adjustment of the Contract Price equal
to the reasonable and proper additional cost, provided that it has notified the Employer's
Representative under clause 15.

21.2 The Contractor shall bear the cost of complying with any General Change in Law, and shall not be
entitled to any adjustment of the Contract Price or the time for completion in respect of it.

21.3 For the avoidance of doubt, a variation of the Permit, or a change to the emission limits that
apply to energy recovery facilities generally, is a General Change in Law unless it applies expressly to
the Facility and not to other energy recovery facilities.

## Schedule 1: Scope of the Works

1. Boilers: replacement of superheater, economiser and evaporator pressure parts on each Line, phased over the Planned Outages.
2. Grates and feed: refurbishment of the grate bars, hydraulic drives and feed chutes on each Line.
3. Flue gas treatment: replacement of the bag filter housings and bags, renewal of the reagent dosing systems, and renewal of the selective non-catalytic reduction (SNCR) system on each Line.
4. Turbine and generator: two major overhauls of the steam turbine and generator, with the condenser retube.
5. Control systems: migration of the distributed control system and the continuous emissions monitoring interface to the current platform.
6. Ash handling: refurbishment of the bottom ash extractors and conveyors and the fly ash silos.
7. Civils and building fabric: tipping hall floor, bunker walls (excluding repairs instructed as Variations), cladding and roofing.

## Schedule 2: Planned Outages and Phases

The Works are delivered in 19 Phases. Phase 1 is the Line 2 outage in the autumn of 2025. From 2026 to 2034, Line 1 is taken out in the spring and Line 2 in the autumn of each year. Each Planned Outage lasts six weeks unless otherwise agreed.

| Phase | Line | Planned Outage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Line 2 | September to October 2025 |
| 2 | Line 1 | March to April 2026 |
| 3 | Line 2 | September to October 2026 |
| 4 | Line 1 | April to May 2027 |
| 5 to 19 | Alternating | Autumn (Line 2) and spring (Line 1), September 2027 to October 2034 |
