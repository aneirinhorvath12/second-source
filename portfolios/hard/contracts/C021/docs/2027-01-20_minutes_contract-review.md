Cost to date £31,862,450. Transaction price £148,300,000. Estimate to complete £113,700,000. Forecast final cost £145,562,450. Forecast margin £2,737,550 (1.8%).

Phase 3 (Line 2) was completed and the Line returned on time. Phases 1 to 3 are complete.

## 2. Estimate to complete

Owen Tasker took the meeting through the ETC build-up. The emissions compliance works are included at the most likely value in EC-02 (£18.4m). They are partly offset by programme efficiencies of £7.9m identified through the FY27 business plan. Workstreams are being developed, with owners to be confirmed after the Line 1 outage.

Alan Whitcombe asked whether the efficiencies were achievable. Fiona Ashdown said the programme has eight years to run and the efficiencies are in line with what the division has delivered on comparable contracts. The scope of the emissions works will be confirmed by the Line 1 survey in April 2027.
