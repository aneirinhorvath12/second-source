# Internal memo

**To:** Martin Keale, Programme Director
**From:** Rhys Gethin, Site and Outage Manager
**Date:** 25 September 2026
**Subject:** Near-miss report: grating removed without edge protection, Line 2 FGT hall

**Date and time:** Friday 18 September, 10:45.

**What happened:** A grating panel at level 3 of the Line 2 FGT hall was lifted before the temporary edge protection was fitted. A Fellgate supervisor noticed and stopped the work. Nobody was injured.

**Immediate actions:** Area barriered; edge protection fitted; crew rebriefed.

**Proposed actions:** Grating removal to be added to the permit-to-work checklist; Fellgate to rebrief all crews.
