**Held:** 22 January 2026
**Present:** Alan Whitcombe (Divisional Finance Director, chair), Fiona Ashdown (Commercial Director), Martin Keale (Programme Director), Priya Raman (Commercial Manager), Owen Tasker (Project Controls Manager)

## 1. Position at 31 December 2025

Cost to date £13,940,210. Transaction price £147,440,000 (Contract Price plus VO-001). Forecast final cost £133,610,000. Forecast margin £13,830,000 (9.4%), in line with the tender margin.

Phase 1 (Line 2, September to October 2025) was completed and the Line returned on time. The superheater pressure parts for Phases 2 to 5 have been ordered and the first batch is in store.

- VO-001 Replacement of the tipping hall roller shutters and controls (EI-004): £1,240,000, accepted 21 October 2025. Included.
- EI-009 turbine hall crane inspection and re-rating: instruction received; quotation being prepared. Not included.

## 3. Risks and opportunities

The regulator issued a consultation in November 2025 on revised emission limits for energy recovery facilities. Helen Mabry's view is that the proposals are at an early stage and may change before they are confirmed. No allowance has been made in the forecast. Martin Keale will keep the consultation under review and report at each quarterly review.

The main delivery risk remains the condition of the Line 1 economiser, which will be opened up in Phase 2. The P50 risk allowance of £2,150,000 is unchanged.
