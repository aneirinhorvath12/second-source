# Internal memo

**To:** Martin Keale, Programme Director
**Cc:** Owen Tasker, Project Controls Manager
**From:** Helen Mabry, Engineering Manager
**Date:** 08 July 2026
**Subject:** Carnbrook: screening of the revised emission limits

Martin,

As requested, a first look at what the revised limits mean for Carnbrook.

**NOx.** The new daily average limit is well below what either Line achieves today. The SNCR renewal in our scope (Schedule 1, item 3) will not get there on its own. The realistic options are a tail-end SCR on each Line, with reheat downstream of the bag filters, or a combined approach with a catalytic filter. Both need new reactors, ductwork, fans and controls.

**Other species.** The revised limits for dust, HCl and SO2 look achievable with the bag filter and dosing works already in our scope, subject to the commissioning results.

**Order of magnitude.** On a very rough basis, £12m to £26m for both Lines, installed during the Planned Outages. The width reflects how little we know about:

- the condition of the ductwork between the bag filter outlets and the stacks, which has not been opened up since the Facility was built;
- whether the FGT hall roof steelwork can take the reactor and duct loads, or needs strengthening;
- the space available for the reheat exchangers.

**Next steps.** I recommend a concept study (EC-02), to be issued in October, and an intrusive survey of the duct runs and the hall steel on each Line during its next Planned Outage. The earliest outage that can be planned in time for the survey is Line 1 in April 2027 (Phase 4).

Helen
